# Acceptance: objects in the window (M8.5 step 2b)

This is the script for accepting M8.5 step 2b (`tasks/plans/m8.5-objects.md`): the objects of 2a seen, walked
against and used in the window, with the mouse first. You walked each item as it landed (2026-10-15 and 16);
this walk puts them together. Built in five items:
1. **Shapes:** objects and piles drawn in the view as lines over dark fills.
2. **The mirror:** the window refuses a step into an object, as the engine does.
3. **The verbs screen:** "Within reach", a button per thing you can do, greyed out with the reason.
4. **The inventory:** Drop, the pile underfoot with Pick up, an open container with Take out and Put in.
5. **The lines:** every object event has a line in the log, each object by its name.

Added on the way at your request (2026-10-16): **the outcome notice.** The words of a command you started
stand centred, are repeated in the log, and fade as you walk away; the bottom message line is retired.

**Departures from the plan** (each in the plan's "Decided by me" section):
- Use opens the verbs screen after checking in advance whether the engine would ask which or refuse, so
  nothing refused is sent.
- Search here is never Shift+Space's default (it costs ten minutes).
- Lore is named `lore`, not `story` (M11's word). The notice keeps its six lines.
- A flag a verb needs is not named: "something else must be done first".
- The ground's button reads "Pick up" (the stores already say Take); Put in names its container.

**Start.** Run `just run --windowed --pack packs/base --pack packs/test --pack crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/objects`,
start a new game with devtools on, make a party with a rogue and a wizard, and teleport to `objects:map:yard` (Esc, Debug, the World page). Each
"stand at" below is a teleport there, or a walk. Watch the overlay's "refusals 0  disagreements 0" throughout.

## Part 1: seeing and walking
1. Stand at (0, 2) facing East and look around with the mouse.
   *You will see* the crate north of you at (1, 1), the statue at (3, 0), the plaque on the edge at (4, 1),
   the arrow pile at (2, 2), the shut gate across the hedge's gap, and beyond the hedge the fountain, the
   chest and the torch. No lever and no rune: they are hidden.
2. Walk into the crate from (1, 2) going North, then south from (3, 1) into the gap.
   *You will see* the party stop at the side of its cell and slide, as on a wall; the shut gate holds it.
   Refusals stay 0.

## Part 2: using things
3. Stand at (4, 1) facing South and press Shift+Space.
   *You will see* "Here the yard. Mind the loft." in a panel in the middle and in the log. Turn on the spot:
   it stays. Step North: it fades as you start and is gone by the cell's edge, and stays gone when you come
   back. Press Shift+Space again and click the "x" at the panel's top right: it closes, and the log keeps it.
4. Stand at (3, 1) facing South and press Space, then "Use...".
   *You will see* "Within reach" with the gate's Push greyed out: "something else must be done first".
5. Stand at (0, 1) facing North.
   *You will see* "Spotted: Lever" at once if your best passive Perception reaches 12. Otherwise press Space,
   "Search here", until it is found: each search says "{name} searches: Investigation {n}", and the find
   says "Found: Lever" and the lever's lore, "Rust flakes from the handle; it was pulled often once.". The
   lever is drawn once found.
6. Use the lever, go back to (3, 1) facing South, and Push the gate from the verbs screen.
   *You will see* "Something grinds beyond the wall.", then "Gate is now open" and "The gate swings open.",
   and you walk through the gap.

## Part 3: containers and the ground
7. Stand at (5, 4) facing North and open the verbs (Space, "Use...").
   *You will see* "Chest  closed" with "Force it open  Athletics DC 15  2 min" and "Pick the lock  Sleight of
   Hand DC 10  5 min" (a rogue carries thieves' tools). Press one until it holds: each try says the check, e.g.
   "{rogue} Sleight of Hand: 14 against 10, success"; a failure adds "Chest: Open failed" and "The lid will not
   move.". Success says "Chest is now open" and "The lid comes up.".
8. Select the rogue in the party column and open Inventory.
   *You will see* "Chest" under the stores with "Dagger  Take out", and "Put in Chest" on each kit row. Take
   the dagger out and put it back; the log says "{rogue} takes Dagger out" and "{rogue} puts Dagger in".
9. Close the inventory, open the verbs, and press the chest's Search.
   *You will see* "Found 25 gp 0 sp 0 cp" and "Coins glint at the bottom.". The Search is then greyed out:
   "done already".
10. Stand at (2, 2), open Inventory, and press Pick up on "Arrow x10"; then Drop on one of the rogue's daggers.
    *You will see* "Arrow x9", then the dagger in "On the ground", and the lines "… picks up Arrow" and
    "… drops Dagger". The pile is drawn under you. Close the inventory and press Space, "Search here": the
    notice says the roll, the bootprints' lore ("Old bootprints in the mud lead toward the gap.") and "On the
    ground: Arrow x9, Dagger".

## Part 4: searching and lore (owner, 2026-10-16)
11. Stand at (1, 2) facing North and search ("Search here").
    *You will see* the crate's lore, "Stencilled on a slat: LOFT STORES.", and "Within reach: Crate". Every
    search tells it again.
12. Stand at (5, 4) facing North and search.
    *You will see* "Within reach: Chest (closed)" (or "(open)" after Part 3) and, with no lore and nothing
    hidden here, "Nothing hidden here".
13. Stand at (4, 3) facing West and search until the roll reaches 10.
    *You will see* "Low on a hedge root, someone carved: B. R. was here." once; later searches don't repeat it,
    even after a save and a load.
14. Stand at (3, 1) facing North (the statue) and search until the roll reaches 12.
    *You will see* "Under the moss on the plinth, a name: Ser Aldric." once. A lower roll tells nothing: the
    statue's own lore stands in for its kind's "Moss covers the plinth.".

## Part 5: finding and spending
15. Stand at (0, 2) facing East and press Shift+Space on the loose stone.
    *You will see* "Found Gem (50 gp); into the stores", "Under the stone lies a gem." and "Loose stone is
    gone"; the stone is no longer drawn.
16. Stand at (1, 4) facing North, open the verbs, and press "Toss in a gem and wish" until it holds.
    *You will see* each try's Wisdom check; a failure says "Fountain: Use failed" and "The water stays still.
    You fish the gem back out."; success says "Gem (50 gp) from the stores is used up", "Found 1 gp 0 sp 0 cp"
    and "Silver coins bob up where the gem sank.".
17. Stand at (6, 0) facing North; select the wizard, open Spells, and cast Detect Magic.
    *You will see* "Sensed by magic: Rune": the rune at (2, 4) is found behind the hedge and drawn.
18. Stand at (6, 3) facing South, open the door with Use, then open the verbs.
    *You will see* "Close the door" first, then the torch's Extinguish. Press it: "Torch is now out".

## Part 6: the full log (owner, 2026-10-17)
19. After a few searches and a fight, press L.
    *You will see* the Log, most of the window wide, scrolled to the newest line, each line stamped "Day N
    hh:mm", the fight's own lines among them. The running log is hidden under it.
20. Type "found" into the filter.
    *You will see* only the lines holding it, and "N of M lines"; typing L does not close it. Esc closes it.
21. Press the "Log  L" button, then Esc; click the running log at the top left.
    *You will see* the Log open each time.
