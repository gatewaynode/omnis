# Acceptance: the attribute bar and the forms (M5.6)

The sixth step of M5 Editor v1 (`tasks/plans/m5-editor-v1.md` row 6): the attributes every cell has, always in
reach over the plan, and the forms for placements, terrains, levels and notes. Built in three commits (6a the
bar, 6b the placements, 6c terrains, levels and notes). No preview yet (M5.7), no Playtest (M5.8).

**A Save writes the pack on disk and reseals it.** Hand-test on a scratch copy, never on `packs/test` itself:
`cp -R packs/test /tmp/omnis-scratch` and name `/tmp/omnis-scratch` as the last pack.

## What was built

### 6a. The attribute bar (2026-10-24)
- **The bar** stands over the plan, between the tools and the inspector, and reads the cursor's cell:
  `Cell (x, y)  level N`, then **Terrain** (the map's terrains and "no ground", stepped with the arrows, round),
  **Height** (the cell's own height in eighths, −64 to 64, stepped by one) and, on a stair cell only, **Rise**
  (the way the stair climbs). Each step paints the cell at once and is one undo step; the brushes in the tools
  column are untouched (the Height brush still paints many cells at a set height). The owner's direction after
  the M5.5 hand test: the height is stepped where it is, wherever the tools column is scrolled.
- **Words in place.** A column whose words change but whose shape does not (a choice stepped, the inspector's
  lines, the title's `*`) keeps its nodes and has its words rewritten; only a change of shape (a control dimmed
  or woken, a row added) rebuilds it. Each column's words are its own (`OfScreen`), so the bar's title never
  lands on the inspector's. This is what lets a form's text field (6b) keep its caret while the words around it
  change.
- **The keyboard.** A rebuilt column gives the keyboard back to the field that had it, and to the first field
  of a column drawn anew with fields; a column without fields never takes it. A click on the plan while a field
  has the keyboard takes it back: clicking elsewhere is the way out of a field.
- **The kit.** `Kind::Text` has `lines`: above one, Enter breaks a line and that many lines show (the notes'
  widget, 6c). An editor text field carries the editor's marker, never the game screens'.

### 6b. The forms and the placements (2026-10-24)
- **The inspector** now reads, after the cell's lines: the map's start with **Set start here** (dim when the
  cursor is the start facing the same way, or off the ground level); one row per placement on the cursor's
  cell (`Portal to …`, `Encounter …`, `Site …`, `Object … (kind)`, `Ground pile`, `Lore …`), each with **Edit**
  and **Remove** (one undo step); **Add** with a choice of kind (Portal, Encounter, Site, Object, Ground pile,
  Lore) and **Add here**; then the map's own: the random encounter table (**Edit**) and the rest events
  (**Add**, each with Edit and Remove).
- **A form** opens at the top of the inspector: a heading saying what and where, its fields one to a row (text
  fields, choices with arrows, numbers with arrows and a slider, yes/no as a choice), its lists (**Add row**,
  each row's fields and **Remove**), then **Apply**, **Cancel** and, when it edits something already there,
  **Remove**. Apply makes one undo step and closes the form; a field that does not read (an id of two words,
  a missing marker, dice not like `2d6`) is said in the tools column and the form stands. Opening a form over
  an open one replaces it, said in the tools column. A portal, a site and a pile are placed on the cell the
  form opened at; an encounter, an object and a lore carry their cell and may be moved.
- **The fields.** Portal: to map (a choice of the loaded maps), to level, to x, to y, facing, marker.
  Encounter: id, x, y, disposition, once, stands apart with its pos and facing, the stacks as rows (creature,
  count). Random table: chance percent, the entries as rows (weight, disposition, creatures as one line
  `goblin 1d4, wolf 2d6`: the one list inside a row that stays text). Site: service (a choice of the loaded
  services). Rest event: terrain (a choice of the map's), rest (Short, Long, Any), chance per mille, text
  key. Object: id, kind (a choice of the loaded object kinds), x, y, pos x and y (the pad, −4..4), facing,
  state, hidden (0 is none), name, lore text, lore DC (0 is none), lore once, the contents as rows (item,
  count). Ground pile: pos x and y, the items as rows. Lore: id, x, y, text key, DC, once.
- **The keyboard.** A form just opened puts the caret in its first text field; typing goes to the form (its
  words are the field's own, so nothing redraws); the arrows and a slider step the other fields; a click on
  the plan takes the keyboard back and the form stands; Enter in a one-line field does nothing (Apply is
  the button). The loader's verdict follows every Apply: an id nothing answers to is listed as an error and
  Save stays dim, as with a paint.

### 6c. Terrains, levels and notes (2026-10-24)
- **Terrains.** Under the brushes, **Add terrain** opens the form of a new terrain and **Edit terrain** the
  form of the brush's terrain (dim with "choose a terrain brush" otherwise): glyph (one character), name,
  floor, ceiling and block surfaces (empty is none), passable, opaque, red, green and blue, visibility depth
  (1 to 32), step minutes, climb (eighths), join (none, Up, Down, Ladder, Hole). Apply adds the terrain, or
  replaces the edited one (a new glyph renames it on every tile; a changed join is refused while tiles use
  it); **Remove terrain** is refused while any tile uses it, in the document's words. The palette follows.
- **Levels.** On the Level row, **Add level** (the first free number above the ground offered; its name key;
  its base in eighths) and **Level…** for the shown level's name and base, with **Remove level**, which drops
  its tiles and placements; dim on the ground level ("the ground level is the map"). The Level choice follows.
- **Notes.** The inspector reads `Note: <first line, or none>` for the cursor's cell with **Edit note**, and in
  the map section `Map note` and `Level note` with Edit. The form is one field of four lines (Enter breaks a
  line) under "Written to the pack at once; not undone." Apply writes `notes/<map name>.ron` in the pack at
  once; the title shows no `*` and Undo does not take it back; **Clear note** removes it (an emptied file is
  deleted). Keys: `<map>/<level>/<x>,<y>`, `<map>/level/<n>`, `<map>`.

## Steps (in the window)
1. `cp -R packs/test /tmp/omnis-scratch`, then
   `just run --windowed --pack packs/base --pack /tmp/omnis-scratch`. Click **Editor**, step the Map choice to
   `test:map:town`, click **Open**.
   *You will see* a one-row bar over the plan: `Cell (10, 2)  level 0`, `Terrain < r street >`,
   `Height < 0 >`. No Rise: the town has no stairs.
2. Click a green cell on the plan, then the bar's Height right arrow three times.
   *You will see* the cell brighten with each step, the bar read 3, the inspector "Height 3 eighths", the title
   gain `*`, Undo wake; the tools column does not move. Click Undo once: 2.
3. Click the bar's Terrain right arrow.
   *You will see* the cell take the next terrain of the map's list and the plan recolour it. Step on past the
   last terrain: "no ground", the cell dark, "No ground" in the inspector. One more: round to the first.
4. Scroll the tools column down to the brushes and step the Height brush's eighths: the column stays put, as
   M5.5 left it; the bar's Height is the cell's, the brush row's is the brush's.
5. (On `crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/levels`, `levels:map:tower`.) Click the stair cell at (2, 0).
   *You will see* `Rise < west >` appear in the bar. Step it: north; the plan's stair mark turns. Click a grass
   cell: Rise is gone.
6. Back on the town, click a green cell, then **Add here** with the Add choice on Portal.
   *You will see* "Portal at (x, y)" at the top of the inspector with its fields, the caret in `marker`. Type
   `signpost`; step `to map` to `test:map:meadow`, `to x` to 16 and `to y` to 30 with the arrows (or drag the
   slider); click **Apply**. The form closes, the inspector lists "Portal to test:map:meadow" with Edit and
   Remove, the title shows `*`. **Undo** takes it back; **Redo** returns it.
7. Click **Edit** on that portal: the form comes back filled, with **Remove** beside Cancel. Change the marker,
   Apply. Click **Remove**: the row is gone.
8. Step Add to Encounter, **Add here**. Type `two words` as the id, click Apply.
   *You will see* the form stand and the tools column say an id is one word. Type `rats`, click **Add row**
   under Stacks, type `test:monster:giant_rat` and step count to 2, Apply. The inspector lists "Encounter
   rats" and the loader still takes the map. Type a creature nothing answers to in another encounter: after
   Apply the inspector lists the loader's error and Save is dim with it; Undo clears it.
9. Click **Set start here** on a cell: the Start line follows and the plan's start mark moves. Click **Edit**
   by "No random encounters", **Add row**, type `test:monster:giant_rat 1d2` as the creatures, step chance
   percent to 5, Apply: "Random encounters: 1 entry at 5%". Click **Edit** on a rest event and step its chance;
   Apply.
10. With a form open and the caret in a field, click the plan.
    *You will see* the cursor move and the form stay; the keys move the cursor again (the field has let go).
11. Click **Add terrain** (under the brushes). Type `m`, `mud`, `grass` for the glyph, name and floor, drag red
    to about 90, step join to Up, Apply.
    *You will see* `m mud` among the brushes; paint a cell with it. Choose it and click **Edit terrain**: the
    form filled; set the glyph to `r` and Apply: refused, the glyph is taken, the form stands; rename it to
    `marsh`, Apply: the brush row says so. **Remove terrain** on the painted terrain: refused while in use;
    Undo the paint and remove it: gone.
12. Click **Add level**: level 1 offered; type `test:text:map.town.loft` as the name, Apply.
    *You will see* the Level choice step to 1 (an empty level, all no ground). Paint a cell. Click **Level…**,
    step base, Apply; **Remove level**: the choice shows 0 again.
13. Click **Edit note** by `Note: none`, type two lines, Apply.
    *You will see* "Note: <the first line>" in the inspector, no `*` in the title, Undo unchanged, and
    `/tmp/omnis-scratch/notes/town.ron` holding the note. Map note and Level note likewise. Edit note again,
    **Clear note**: "Note: none".
14. **Save**, then New game: the terrain you painted and the portal you placed are walked.

## Known and expected
- A height outside −64..64 (the file allows ±4096) reads as the nearest end of the bar's choice and a step
  from there goes to 63 or −63.
- A text field's limit counts characters on screen and bytes in the form; they differ only for text beyond
  ASCII, where the data layer's own limit decides on Apply.
- Moving the cursor while a form is open redraws the inspector when the new cell's placements differ; the
  form keeps its values, the caret is lost until the field is clicked.
- A random entry's creatures are one typed line (`id dice, id dice`): a list inside a row has no column.
- Editing a rest event's terrain or rest kind makes a new entry (they are its key); remove the old one.
- Map fields (name, kind, tileset, surfaces), Resize and New map have no form yet (M5.10 re-authors through
  ops; a form can follow).
