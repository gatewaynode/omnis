# Acceptance: the title, new game, pause, save and load (parity P2)

The script for your acceptance of parity step P2 (`tasks/plans/p2.md`). The vector client now starts at a
title, starts a game from a form, pauses, saves and loads, and offers ways on when the party falls. Under
it is the shared screen code every later screen is built on (presentation-ARCHITECTURE §6). Character
creation is P3: a new game still takes the fixed party of four.

## Part 1: the title and a new game
1. `just run --windowed`.
   *You will see* "OMNIS" with New game and Load quick save, and Quit in a row of its own. No HUD, no
   buttons in the corners. If there is no `.omnis/quick.ron`, Load reads "Load quick save: no save at
   .omnis/quick.ron" in dim green and does nothing when clicked.
2. Click New game.
   *You will see* "NEW GAME": an underlined Seed field, "blank = random, words are hashed", Saving ‹
   Anywhere ›, Permadeath: Off, Start and Back.
3. Type `dragon` in the Seed field, click the right arrow by Saving twice ("Inns only"), click Permadeath
   (On), then Start.
   *You will see* the town at (10, 2) facing West, the HUD and the buttons back.
   Covered by `tests/screens.rs::the_title_starts_a_game_from_the_form` and
   `the_new_game_form_fills_and_starts_a_game`.

## Part 2: the pause, save and load
1. Press Esc (or the Menu button bottom right).
   *You will see* "PAUSED", the seed (the hash of "dragon"), "Saving: Inns only", "Permadeath: on",
   "Devtools: on", then Resume, Save, Load, Save log, Quit to title, and Quit apart. Save reads "Save: The
   save rule forbids saving here", dim: you are not in an inn. Hold W: nothing moves.
2. Resume, Quit to title from the pause, start a new game with Saving: Anywhere, walk a few cells, pause,
   Save.
   *You will see* "Saved to .omnis/quick.ron" under the buttons.
3. Resume, walk on, pause, Load.
   *You will see* the pause close and the party back where you saved, the HUD line "Loaded .omnis/quick.ron".
   Pause again: Save log now reads that the log no longer replays, naming the save.
   Covered by `the_pause_holds_the_game`, `the_pause_tells_the_game_and_why_saving_is_refused` and
   `a_save_loads_back_and_the_log_stops_replaying`.

## Part 3: a fight and a fall
1. Walk out to the meadow and fight what finds you. In the fight, open Attack's targets, press Esc, then
   Esc again.
   *You will see* the first Esc step the menu back, the second open the pause over the fight; the fight's
   buttons do nothing until you Resume.
   Covered by `in_a_fight_esc_steps_back_first_and_the_pause_holds_the_fight`.
2. If the party falls (or through MCP: `sim_command` with `{"Dev": {"SetHp": ...}}` to 0 for every member, which this devtools world accepts):
   *You will see* "The party has fallen", the fight's last lines, Load last save, Start again and Quit to
   title. Load last save brings back the save from Part 2.
   Covered by `a_fallen_party_loads_its_save_or_starts_again`.

## Part 4: agents
With the game at the title, ask for `screen_text` through MCP.
*You will see* the title's lines, `[button] "New game"`; and `game_status` answers "no game is running;
start one from the title". Covered by `tests/socket.rs::at_the_title_the_socket_says_no_game_runs_and_reads_the_screen`.
