# Acceptance: pack migrations (M8.5 7h6)

This is the script for accepting M8.5 sub-step 7h6, the sixth of 7h (saves by name; the re-scope is at the top
of `tasks/plans/m8.5-saves-by-name.md`).
- **A pack keeps migrations,** `migrations/NNNN.ron`, sealed with its content, like a database's schema
  migrations. Each names the version it leads **to** (your choice): versions between files need none, and a
  save made at `v` is offered every file with `v < to <=` the pack's version, in order.
- **Six steps.** `Rename`, `Replace` and `Remove` act on names as the save's names are found again (a chain
  composes: `a` to `b`, then `b` to `c`, is `a` to `c`). A removed name leaves every list that holds it, and a
  removed spell gives back its pick. A name the world cannot do without (the party's map, a fight's monster,
  tactics criteria) stays missing, and a race, class, background or region cannot be removed. `MovePlace`,
  `RenameEncounter` and `RenameObject` act on the world after that: the party, a pile, open doors and the
  automap move with a cell; a renamed encounter or object keeps its cleared mark, state and what the party
  knew of it.
- **Checked when a pack loads,** each with its refusal:
  - file names;
  - `to` rising and never past the pack's version;
  - a step names only its own pack's ids, and only what is gone;
  - where a chain leads must exist;
  - a moved cell must lie inside the map;
  - a renamed encounter or object must be there under its new id.
- **Offered only when a step changes the save** (your choice): the verdict `needs_migration`, the Load screen
  titled "These packs moved on", each step in words, **Apply and load** beside **Close**. A save the steps
  find nothing of loads as before, and the files are recorded as applied.
- **Reload too** (your choice): **Reload packs** asks the same way, with **Apply and reload**.
- **Recorded:** the world keeps the files it went through (`migrated`), and its save carries them.
- **Wire, additive:** `save.read` and `pack.reload` gain `migrate` (default false; a bare `pack.reload` still
  reads). `save_check` lists the `migrations`. `PROTOCOL` stays 2; ops 27, tools 27.
- **CLI:**
  - `omnis-cli migration draft --from <old> --to <new> [--pack <dep>]...` writes the next file, with each name
    gone as a `Remove` and the `Rename` to write instead. It never guesses a rename.
  - `omnis-cli save check <save> [--pack <dir>]...` prints the report and fails when the save does not load.

**What moved:** no golden, no shipped pack. A new fixture pack `chain`, in two versions under
`crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/chain/`:
- `v1` (0.1.0): an Old Sword, Junk, an Old Bolt, a Fading Shield and the Chain Hall.
- `v3` (0.3.0): the Sword, the hall's cell (2, 1) turned to trees (the cell moved to (3, 1)), its chest renamed
  `coffer` and its rats `vermin`. Its migrations: `0001.ron` (to 0.2.0) and `0002.ron` (to 0.3.0).

## Steps
The window reloads the same pack folders it started with, so these steps use a copy of `chain` in `/tmp` and
swap its version on disk.

1. Make the copy at 0.1.0:
   ```
   rm -rf /tmp/chain && cp -R crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/chain/v1 /tmp/chain
   ```
2. Start on it:
   ```
   just run --windowed --pack packs/base --pack packs/test --pack crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/objects --pack /tmp/chain
   ```
   Start a **New game**.
3. Give the party what the migrations change. Esc, **Debug**, the **Party** page:
   - choose **Item** **Old Sword**, Count 1, **To stores**;
   - then **Item** **Junk**, Count 1, **To stores**.

   On the **World** page choose the map **chain:map:hall (5 by 3)**, x 2, y 1, and press **Go**.
   *You will see* the party in a small grassy hall. Its stores hold the Old Sword and the Junk.
4. Save: Esc, the pause screen's **Save**.
5. Move the pack on to 0.3.0. In a terminal:
   ```
   rm -rf /tmp/chain && cp -R crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/chain/v3 /tmp/chain
   ```
6. The reload asks. Esc, **Debug**, the **World** page, **Reload packs**.
   *You will see* the Load screen:
   - "These packs moved on";
   - "The reloaded packs' migrations change the running world. Apply them to reload; nothing changes until
     then.";
   - "Packs changed:", then "chain: running 0.1.0, reloaded 0.3.0";
   - "Migrations:", the eight steps, from "chain 0001.ron: item 'chain:item:old_sword' is now
     'chain:item:mid_sword'" to "chain 0002.ron: map 'chain:map:hall': encounter 'rats' is now 'vermin'";
   - **Apply and reload** and **Close**.
7. Choose **Close**.
   *You will see* the Debug page. Nothing changed: the stores still hold the Old Sword and the Junk.
8. Press **Reload packs** again, then **Apply and reload**.
   *You will see* the Debug page, and the log reads "Packs reloaded: their migrations applied. The world has
   moved on". The stores hold the **Sword** and no Junk. The party stands one cell east, at (3, 1): its old
   cell is trees now, and the cell moved with the party.
9. Load the save made at 0.1.0: Esc, the pause screen's **Load**.
   *You will see* the Load screen again, with "These packs moved on", "chain: saved 0.1.0, loaded 0.3.0" and
   **Apply and load**. Choose **Apply and load**.
   *You will see* the game, the log line ending "made with older packs, their migrations applied. The world
   has moved on", the Sword in the stores and the party at (3, 1).
10. Save again, then **Load**.
    *You will see* it load at once: the save now names 0.3.0, so nothing is offered.
11. Optional, by MCP: ask me for `save_check` on the quick save of step 4 (before step 10 overwrites it), or
    for `pack_reload` before step 8.
    *You will see* the verdict `needs_migration` and the `migrations` listed. Asked again with `migrate`, the
    answer is the load, or `done`.
12. Optional, the CLI on the repository's fixture:
    ```
    cargo run -p omnis-cli -- save check .omnis/quick.ron --pack packs/base --pack packs/test --pack crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/objects --pack /tmp/chain
    ```
    *You will see* "verdict: Same" after step 10. `migration draft` is
    covered by its test, which drafts 0.1.0 to a copy of 0.3.0 without its migrations.
13. Quit, and remove the copy: `rm -rf /tmp/chain`. Nothing in the repository changed (`git status --short`
    lists only your untracked files).

## What the tests cover
- **`omnis-data/tests/migrations.rs` (7):**
  - both versions load, and only 0.3.0 has migrations;
  - the files offered after each saved version, and none for a later save, a version not `x.y.z`, a pack
    with none, or a file past the pack's version;
  - versions compared part by part;
  - a chain composes its names;
  - each of 19 checks refused with its row on a resealed scratch copy;
  - a rename from what is still there;
  - a migration sealed with its pack.
- **`omnis-sim/tests/migrations.rs` (11):**
  - a save the migrations change asks first, both files offered and nothing missing, refused unmigrated
    with the files named;
  - applied, every step did its part: the sword renamed twice, the junk gone from the stores and the chest,
    the old bolt now the fire bolt and known once, the fading spell gone, picks given back, the party, its
    pile and automap tile moved with the cell (the tile's own mark arriving), the chest's state and the party's notice under `coffer`, the
    rats cleared as `vermin`, the files recorded; it folds, and its next save loads as the same world with
    what it went through;
  - a save they find nothing of loads by name with every view the same, the files recorded;
  - a name step alone, and a place step alone, each asks first;
  - a cell moved on packs whose names are all the same asks first and moves the party;
  - the same version is offered nothing;
  - a removed map the party stands on is missing, with why;
  - tactics: a removed spell's action leaves its runbook, while criteria naming it are missing;
  - a reload asks first, changing nothing, then applies as a load does (the same world);
  - `restore` needs consent.
- **Client (`omnis-vector/tests/load_report.rs`, 2 more):**
  - a save the migrations change asks, Close loading nothing, then Apply and load loads it;
  - Reload packs asks, then Apply and reload reloads and goes back to the Debug page.

  The socket reads a bare `pack.reload`.
- **CLI (`omnis-cli/tests/cli.rs`, 3 more; `headless.rs`):**
  - `migration draft` writes each name gone, never a guessed rename, and the draft loads once resealed;
  - a race gone is left open, a commented `Rename`, never a `Remove`;
  - the headless driver's `save.read` asks first, then loads with `migrate`;
  - `save check` prints the report, and fails on packs without `chain`.
- **MCP:** `migrate` is optional on the wire; the tools' examples carry it.
