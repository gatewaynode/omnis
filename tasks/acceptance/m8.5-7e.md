# Acceptance: the fold (M8.5 7e)

This is the script for accepting M8.5 sub-step 7e (`tasks/plans/m8.5-save-model.md`). A **fold** makes what the
world holds into its new bases. The journal's interactions move into the **archive**, the history (7f will
read it), and the journal starts again empty. The player is told: "The world has moved on". Bases and archive
segments are never overwritten, so **an older save loads at its own moment**.

**When it folds:**
- **Automatically**, at a safe point (a long rest done, a room taken at the inn, coming into a settlement
  region) once the journal holds 5,000 interactions (owner, 2026-10-18). It is a rules value, `fold_interactions`,
  and no pack sets it yet. At about 300 bytes an interaction that is some 1.5 MB, or about 1.5 million commands of
  play, so it cannot be reached by hand; the tests set it low.
- **By hand**, with the debug page's **Fold** button (`DevCommand::Fold`), outside a fight.

**Tidy** lists the world-folder files that no save under `.omnis/` names: bases, archive segments, and whole
folders of worlds no save names. It deletes them only when asked, with the debug page's **Delete them** or
`omnis-cli world tidy --yes`. A save under `.omnis/` that does not read stops the tidy, so its files are never
taken. A schema 7 save (a whole world) names nothing and is passed over.

**Start.** Run `just run --windowed --pack packs/base --pack packs/test --pack
crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/objects`, start a new game with devtools on, and make a party.

## Steps
1. Esc, Debug, the World page: teleport to `objects:map:yard`, cell (0, 1), facing North. The lever at (0, 0) is
   hidden: **Search** until it is drawn. Esc and **Save**, then in a terminal `cp .omnis/quick.ron
   .omnis/before-lever.ron`.
   *You will see* the World page say "Journal: 1 interaction since the last fold; archive: 0 segments" (the
   yard's time caught up when you arrived).
2. **Use** the lever (pulled). Teleport to (3, 2) facing South and **Use** the gate (it opens). On the World page,
   press **Fold**.
   *You will see* "The world has moved on" in the log, and the page say "Journal: 0 interactions since the last
   fold; archive: 1 segment".
3. **Save**, then `cp .omnis/quick.ron .omnis/after-fold.ron` and `ls -R .omnis/worlds`.
   *You will see*, in the world's folder, `regions/objects/yard/<hash>.ron` (the yard as it now stands),
   `world/<hash>.ron` (the lever's flag, a world flag) and `archive/<hash>.ron` (the three interactions folded
   away).
4. `cp .omnis/before-lever.ron .omnis/quick.ron`, then Esc and **Load**.
   *You will see* "Loaded .omnis/quick.ron: 1 interaction applied in T ms", the lever up (and still drawn, since
   you had found it) and the gate shut: the older save at its own moment.
5. `cp .omnis/after-fold.ron .omnis/quick.ron`, then **Load**.
   *You will see* "0 interactions applied", with the lever down and the gate open.
6. On the World page press **Tidy**.
   *You will see* "Every world file is named by a save", or only folders left over from earlier walks that no
   save names now (the 7d walk's converted world, say). **Delete them** is dim until Tidy has found something.
7. `cp .omnis/before-lever.ron .omnis/quick.ron` and `rm .omnis/after-fold.ron`. No save names the folded yard
   now. Press **Tidy** again, and in a terminal run `cargo run -p omnis-cli -- world tidy`.
   *You will see* both list the yard base, the world base and the archive segment. **Delete them**, and
   `ls -R .omnis/worlds` shows them gone, with the manifest kept. **Load** still brings back the before-lever moment.

## What the tests cover
- **`omnis-sim/tests/fold.rs` (10):**
  - a fold makes the world its bases, empties the journal, archives the entries and says how many;
  - folding again with nothing journaled archives nothing;
  - a fold is refused in a fight;
  - two folds and a save after each, and both saves load at their own moment;
  - a save names its archive, the segment holds the folded interactions, and a segment known by name is not
    written again;
  - the threshold defaults in code;
  - a long rest folds at the threshold and not one short of it;
  - a short rest is not a safe point;
  - coming into a settlement folds and a wild region does not;
  - the inn's room folds;
  - tidy lists only the unnamed base, a whole unnamed world, and never a stranger.
- **`omnis-data/tests/files.rs`:** listing never follows a link or leaves the root, and deleting takes a plain file
  only.
- **`omnis-vector/tests/game_io.rs`:** tidy lists and deletes under a root, and a broken save stops it.
- **`omnis-vector/tests/debug.rs`:** Fold folds and says so; Delete is dim until Tidy found something.
- **`omnis-cli/tests/cli.rs`:** `world tidy` lists, then deletes with `--yes`.
- **Mutations:** 15 in the engine (one exposed a guard nothing can reach, now removed) and 6 in the hosts.
