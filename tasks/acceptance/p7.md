# Acceptance: the fight screen (parity P7)

The script for your acceptance of parity step P7 (`tasks/plans/p7.md`). The fight's controls are now a
screen drawn with the others' renderer:
- **The action column:** the budget line, a "Reactions < on >" switch, and Features as its own entry.
- **The pickers:** spells, features and items open in a panel over the picture (your four answers of
  2026-10-11).
- **Lit by the engine:** every row takes its cost and its refusal from the engine's views. The engine
  now says what each fight spell spends of the turn, and which of the acting member's items can be
  used, with the reason the others cannot.

The fixed party: Brenna (fighter), Durin (cleric), Ilvara (wizard), Pip (rogue). The start is the town
at (10, 2), facing West.

## Before the fight (in town)
1. `just run --windowed --seed 1`.
2. Through MCP, `party_get` for the members' `id`s.
3. Give Durin a spyglass: `sim_command` with `{"Dev": {"GiveItem": {"member": <Durin's id>, "item":
   "base:item:spyglass", "count": 1}}}`.
4. Walk onto the guild (10, 1) and learn Shield for Ilvara, as in P6's script (`{"Dev": {"SetGold":
   {"gold": 100000}}}` first if Learn is dim for money). Leave.
5. Optional, for Cunning Action, which a rogue has from level 2:
   - `{"Dev": {"SetXp": {"member": <Pip's id>, "xp": 300}}}`;
   - on the trainer (7, 1), train Pip.

## Part 1: the turn
1. Walk out of town until monsters are met.
   *You will see* "Monsters ahead: hostile" (or the group's mood) in the action column, with Fight,
   Bribe (N gold), Hide and Run.
   Covered by `tests/combat_menu.rs::the_encounter_offers_its_four_choices`.
2. Click Fight, then click Dodge on each member's turn until Durin's comes.
   *You will see* on Durin's turn:
   - "Round R: Durin's turn" heading the action column;
   - under it "Action 1   Bonus 1   Reaction 1 (on)";
   - "Reactions < on >";
   - "1  Attack", "2  Cast", "Features: has no features" (dim), "4  Use", "5  Dodge", "6  Swap",
     "7  End turn", "8  Flee".

   Covered by `tests/fight_screen.rs::the_turn_offers_features_beside_the_others` and
   `the_budget_line_reads_the_acting_member_s_turn`.
3. Click ">" on Reactions.
   *You will see* it read "off", the budget line end "(off)", and "Durin's reactions are off" among the
   HUD's lines; it is still Durin's turn, his action and bonus action unspent. Click again: "on".
   Covered by `react_flips_the_acting_member_s_reactions_and_costs_nothing` and
   `tests/combat.rs::the_reactions_switch_turns_them_off_and_the_turn_goes_on`.

## Part 2: the pickers
1. On Durin's turn, click Cast.
   *You will see* a panel over the picture, the action column and the monsters:
   - "Durin casts which spell?";
   - "SP 2/2   Action 1   Bonus 1   Reaction 1 (on)";
   - a row per spell, "Guidance  0 pt" through "Cure Wounds  1 pt";
   - "Back".

   The roll log and the party column stay in view.
   Covered by `tests/combat.rs::cast_opens_the_picker_over_the_picture_and_esc_closes_it`.
2. Press Esc.
   *You will see* the panel close, the picture and the turn's list back.
3. Click Cast, then Bless.
   *You will see* the panel close and "Durin casts Bless on whom?" in the column, with the members
   clickable on the field. Click Brenna. Then click End turn (his bonus action is left), and Dodge
   until Durin's next turn.
4. Click Cast.
   *You will see* "in force" after Bless.
   Covered by `the_spell_notes_go_reaction_then_the_refusal_then_in_force_then_bonus_action` (which also
   checks "bonus action" after Healing Word and "need 1 pt" with no points).
5. On Ilvara's turn, click Cast.
   *You will see* "Shield  1 pt: reaction" dim, with "declare it in tactics" after it.
6. On Durin's turn, click Use.
   *You will see* "Potion of healing" (if he carries one) and "Spyglass: not from a fight" dim; no
   features among them.
   Covered by `use_lists_only_items_with_the_engine_s_refusal` (with the action spent: "the turn's
   action is spent", and Use dim "nothing to use now").
7. On Brenna's turn, click Features.
   *You will see* "Second Wind" with "1 use, bonus action" after it. Click it.
   *You will see* her healing in the log. It is still her turn. Features is now dim: "no feature can
   be used now".
   Covered by `second_wind_reads_its_uses_and_cost_and_dims_once_spent`.
8. (If Pip trained) On Pip's turn, click Features.
   *You will see* "Cunning Action: swap" and "Cunning Action: hide", each "at will, bonus action".
   Swap asks "Pip swaps places with whom (Cunning Action)?" and offers the others.
   Covered by `cunning_action_splits_into_swap_and_hide`.

## Part 3: the end
1. Fight on until the monsters fall.
   *You will see* "Victory! N XP each, G gold" in the HUD's lines and as the log's last line.
   Covered by `tests/labels.rs` (both Victory lines).
2. In a later fight, open a step (Attack), then let a turn pass another way (an MCP `sim_command` with
   `{"Combat": "Dodge"}`).
   *You will see* the column back at the turn's list for whoever acts now, not the old step.
   Covered by `tests/combat.rs::a_turn_that_passed_elsewhere_takes_the_menu_back_to_the_top` (red
   before the fix) and `the_menu_goes_back_to_the_top_when_the_turn_passes`.
