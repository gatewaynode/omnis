# Acceptance: camera pitch (M8.5 step 4b)

This is the script for accepting M8.5 step 4b (`tasks/plans/m8.5-camera-pitch.md`), built to your answers of
2026-10-12:
- **Controls:** the mouse first, keys as well.
- **Limits:** none, a full 360° by 360°.
- **On a step:** without mouse look, the view returns to straight ahead. A setting chooses this.
- **Over the top:** the view is a free head. W, the compass and the party's facing stay with the body.
- **Eye height and field of view:** unchanged, reviewed after this walk.
- **Reduced motion:** my call. The return to level is instant; your own looking is never changed.

Only the client changed. The engine, the save and the protocol did not, and the replays did not move.

## Part 1: in the window
1. Run `just run --windowed --pack packs/base --pack packs/test --pack crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/heights`.
   Start a new game. Use the debug panel (Esc, then Debug, the "World" page) to go to `heights:map:hills` at
   (0, 0) facing East, and close it.
2. Click in the view to start mouse look, and move the mouse down and up.
   *You will see* the view look down at the slope at your feet and up at the amber cliff face and the sky.
3. Keep moving the mouse up, past straight overhead.
   *You will see* the view turn over and look back behind you, upside down. Moving the mouse left and right
   still turns the picture the way the mouse moves. The minimap's marker and the HUD's facing don't change
   when you pitch.
4. With the view still tilted, click again to end mouse look, then press W.
   *You will see* the view return to straight ahead as the party steps.
5. Click to start mouse look, tilt the view, and walk with W while looking.
   *You will see* the view stay tilted as you walk.
6. End mouse look. Press and hold PageUp, then PageDown.
   *You will see* the view tilt up, then down. Press Home: the view is level at once.
7. Tilt the view with PageUp and click the **Level** button at the bottom right (before Camp).
   *You will see* the view level.
8. Open Settings (Esc, then Settings) and step "Level view on step" to "always". Apply. Look with the mouse and
   walk.
   *You will see* the view level on the first step even while the mouse looks. Set it to "never" and apply:
   with mouse look off the view stays tilted as you walk. Set it back to "when not mouse looking".
9. In Settings, turn Reduced motion on and apply. Tilt with PageUp, then step with W.
   *You will see* the view level in one frame, with no ease.
10. Quit, and run `just run --windowed` with no packs named.
    *You will see* the town, the meadow and the dungeon start level and walk as before. In the dungeon, look up
    at the ceiling grid.
