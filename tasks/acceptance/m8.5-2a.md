# Acceptance: objects in data and the engine (M8.5 step 2a)

This is the script for accepting M8.5 step 2a (`tasks/plans/m8.5-objects.md`), built to your answers of
2026-10-14:
- **Objects stand inside a cell:** an offset from the centre in eighths, a facing, a level, a look (box, post,
  slab, arch, sign, heap) and a footprint that may spill into the next cell.
- **The full interaction schema:** states, 13 verbs, requirements (checks, items, flags, a school, a rules slot),
  effects, hidden objects and `once`.
- **Items on the ground:** placed in a map, dropped and picked up, inside containers, and monster loot.
- **Use** does the one thing it can, or lists the choices when there are several.
- **Hidden objects** are found by a sharp eye, by Search, or by a reveal spell; the base pack gains Detect Magic.
- **Test data:** the yard fixture (`crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/objects`). The shipped maps are unchanged.

2a has no buttons or drawings in the window: those are 2b. So this walk runs the game through MCP, the tools
Claude calls for you, while the window shows the event lines.

**Departures from the plan** (each in the plan's "Decided by me" section):
- `Command::Door` was added, so a door listed beside an object can be chosen.
- Search is a plain value beside Interact in the MCP schema, so the command count went to 15, not 16.
- Pick-up works on the party's own cell only, and moving items takes no time.
- Loot rolls on the fight's dice and may hold more kinds than a dropped pile.
- A reveal needs no sight.
- A new wizard starts knowing Detect Magic, which moved the fight replay by that one spell as well as the
  pack hash.

## Part 1: the CLI
1. Run `target/debug/omnis-cli validate packs/base packs/test crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/objects`.
   *You will see* one line ending `21 spells, 8 monsters, 37 rule slots, 7 services, 10 objects`.
2. Run `target/debug/omnis-cli map text objects:map:yard --pack packs/base --pack packs/test --pack
   crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/objects`.
   *You will see* the 7 by 5 yard: the hedge along the south of row 2 with its gap at x 3 (`+-+-+-+ +-+-+-+`),
   the ladder `l` at (6, 1), and the door `=` on the north edge of (6, 4). Objects are not drawn in map text.

## Part 2: the window and MCP
**Start.** Run `just run --windowed --pack packs/base --pack packs/test --pack crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/objects`.
Start a new game with devtools on and make a party. Ask Claude to add a wizard with `party_create`, so the
party has someone who knows Detect Magic. Each step below is a tool call Claude makes. The event lines appear
in the window.

**The ground**
1. Teleport to the yard at (2, 2) facing North (`sim_command` with `Dev Teleport`), then `objects_get`.
   *You will see* `ground` with 10 arrows and no objects in reach.
2. `PickUp` the 10 arrows, then `objects_get`.
   *You will see* "… picks up 10 Arrow" in the window, and `ground` empty.
3. `Drop` 4 arrows, then teleport to (2, 1) facing South and `viewport_get`.
   *You will see* "… drops 4 Arrow", and `piles` with 4 arrows at (2, 2), offset (0, −2): two eighths north of
   the centre, ahead of where you stood.

**A container**
4. Teleport to (5, 4) facing North, then `objects_get`.
   *You will see* `chest_1` closed with no `contents`, and two ways to Open:
   - "force", Athletics DC 15, 2 minutes, no refusal;
   - "pick", Sleight of Hand DC 10, 5 minutes, refused because the party has no thieves' tools.
5. `Act` the chest's Open, option 0, until it opens.
   *You will see* either the lid coming up (`ObjectState` to `open`) or "The lid will not move" with two
   minutes spent; a failure uses up nothing, so you can try again.
6. `objects_get`, then `TakeOut` the dagger and `PutIn` 6 arrows.
   *You will see* the dagger in `contents`, then "… takes Dagger out" and "… puts 6 Arrow in". `objects_get`
   shows the 6 arrows inside, with Search and Close offered.
7. `Act` Search on the chest, then `objects_get`.
   *You will see* `GoldFound` with 2500 copper (25 gold). The chest's Search is still listed, now refused as
   `Spent`.

**Use chooses**
8. Teleport to (6, 3) facing South and `sim_command` `Interact`.
   *You will see* the door on the north edge of (6, 4) open, exactly as before 2a: the torch beyond it was out of
   reach while the door was shut.
9. `Interact` again.
   *You will see* the rejection `ChooseVerb` listing the door and the torch's Extinguish, and nothing changed.
   `Door` closes the door; `Act` on `torch_1` with Extinguish puts the torch out.

**Hidden things and the gate**
10. Teleport to (3, 1) facing South and `Act` the gate's Push.
    *You will see* it refused: `VerbRefused` with `Flag` `objects:flag:lever`. The gate is held shut.
11. Teleport to (0, 1) facing North.
    *You will see*, if anyone's passive Perception is 12 or more, `Noticed` for `lever_1` (`Passive`) at once,
    with no roll: a fighter with Wisdom 10 and Perception has exactly 12. Otherwise `sim_command` `Search` until
    it is found: each search shows `Searched` with an Investigation roll and 10 minutes passing, whether or not
    anything is there, and `Noticed` (`Search`) once a roll reaches 12. `objects_get` lists the lever once found.
12. `Act` Pull on the lever, go back to (3, 1) facing South, and Push the gate again; then step South twice.
    *You will see* "Something grinds beyond the wall", "The gate swings open", and the party walking through
    the gap at (3, 2) to (3, 3). Before the gate opened, that step was blocked by the object.
13. Teleport to (6, 0) facing North and have the wizard `Cast` `base:spell:detect_magic` on themself.
    *You will see* `Noticed` for `rune_1` (`Spell`) four cells off, out of sight behind the hedge. The lever has
    no aura and is never found this way.
14. Teleport to (2, 3) facing South and `Act` Read on the rune.
    *You will see* "A ward against what walks at night": the wizard knows an Abjuration spell (Shield). A party
    with no Abjuration spell is refused with `School`.

**It all keeps**
15. `save_write` to `yard.ron`, then `save_read` it, and `objects_get` at the chest.
    *You will see* the chest open with its 6 arrows, the search spent, the arrows on the ground at (2, 2), the
    gate open and the lever and rune found, all as they were.
16. Quit, and run `just run --windowed` with no packs named.
    *You will see* the town, the meadow and the dungeon play as before. No shipped map holds objects.

## Covered by tests
- **Data** (`omnis-data`):
  - the kinds, placements, piles and loot tables with their limits;
  - every refusal in the bad-pack corpus (`tests/bad_packs.rs`, `bad-objects`), including spills through walls
    and onto no ground, blocking an arrival centre, and a hidden object that blocks;
  - the footprint geometry per facing (`tests/footprint.rs`).
- **Engine** (`omnis-sim/tests`):
  - `objects.rs`: step blocking into, out of and past a cell, a spill, another level, a state that unblocks, and
    sight through footprints;
  - `object_act.rs`: checks, items, flags, schools, rules slots, `once`, and a failure spending only time;
  - `object_use.rs`: Use's routing and `ChooseVerb`;
  - `ground.rs`: Drop, PickUp, TakeOut and PutIn, capacity, a shut chest, and a save round trip;
  - `loot.rs`: loot measured over 4000 seeds, a 1% entry, three picks, victory, flight and defeat;
  - `notice.rs`: the eye at its exact difficulty and range, Search, the reveal's range and Detect Magic;
  - `objects_view.rs`: the view, its agreement with `Act` across the whole yard, and the viewport's objects
    and piles.
- **Protocol:** the MCP schema proof and vocabulary (the yard walked with every view); `docs/api.md`'s counts.
- **Replays:** unmoved through items 1 to 13 and 15. At item 14 they moved for the pack hash and the wizard's
  extra spell alone: with those restored, both reach their old fingerprints.

## Known and expected
- **Nothing is drawn yet.** Objects and piles are not drawn in the window, there are no buttons for verbs or for
  the ground, and found objects and searches have no event lines (2b).
- **Loot** is in the tests only: no fixture fight is placed in the yard, and base monsters carry no loot table.
- **Sight through a gap's corner:** from (2, 1) facing South the party sees (2, 3), (4, 3), (5, 3) and (5, 4)
  behind the hedge, so the chest at (5, 3) is in the viewport. A ray that only touches the corner of the gap
  counts as passing. This was found during the 2a test; it is listed in `horizons.md` and isn't changed here.
- **Detect Magic** isn't sold by the guild yet, and stone, metal and lead don't block it yet.
