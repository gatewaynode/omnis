# Acceptance: inventory, spells on the road, the message line (parity P5)

The script for your acceptance of parity step P5 (`tasks/plans/p5.md`). The party column's Inventory
and Spells now open their screens, and the column stays beside them. Its selection is who receives a
Give, a potion or a spell. Every button is lit by a trial of its exact command, so a button the engine
would refuse is dim with the engine's reason. The cast screen stays open after a cast. The explore
message line shows the last event at the bottom centre.

The fixed party: Brenna (fighter: chain mail, longsword, shield, light crossbow, crossbow bolts), Durin
(cleric), Ilvara (wizard), Pip (rogue). Each carries a holy symbol and a Potion of healing (the acolyte
background). The start is the town at (10, 2), facing West, with the stores empty.

## Part 1: the inventory
1. `just run --windowed --seed 1`. Click Brenna's entry in the party column, then Inventory under it.
   *You will see* "Inventory" in the room left of the column; the column is still shown, not covered.
   The first row reads "Kit of < Brenna >" with Close. Next comes "wearing" and her worn items in slot
   order (main hand, off hand, ranged, body), then "Kit" with a row per item. Each worn item shows its slot and "worn" with Take
   off, Stow and Give. The potion shows "use" with Use, Stow and Give. Under "Stores": the money and
   "food 40", then "Nothing in the stores".
   Covered by `tests/inventory.rs::the_kit_and_the_stores_list_what_the_views_hold` and
   `the_column_opens_the_inventory_beside_it_and_its_selection_receives`.
2. Press W or the movement pad's forward.
   *You will see* nothing move: the party does not walk while the inventory is open.
3. Click Take off on the longsword, then Equip on it.
   *You will see* "Brenna takes off Longsword" under the panels, then "Brenna wields Longsword", and
   the "wearing" line follow.
4. Look at Give on the crossbow bolts with no member selected (click Brenna's entry again to clear the
   selection).
   *You will see* Give dim: "Select a member in the party column". Select Brenna, and it reads "Select
   another member in the party column".
5. Select Pip in the column, then click Give on the crossbow bolts.
   *You will see* "Brenna gives Crossbow bolt to Pip" (one at a time; the counts are 1). The kit
   shown stays Brenna's.
   Covered by `every_live_button_is_taken_and_every_dim_one_says_why` and
   `give_goes_to_the_member_selected_in_the_column`.
6. Click Stow on the holy symbol, then Take on it under "Stores".
   *You will see* "Brenna stows Holy symbol (amulet)", the row under "Stores", then "Brenna takes Holy symbol (amulet)"
   and "Nothing in the stores" again. Take goes to the kit shown, not to the selection.
7. Through MCP, `party_get` for Pip's `id`, then `sim_command` with `{"Dev": {"SetHp": {"member": <id>,
   "hp": 2}}}`. With Pip still selected, click Use on Brenna's potion.
   *You will see* "Brenna uses Potion of healing on Pip", then "Pip regains N hp, now H", and Pip's HP
   go up in the column. With no selection, Use heals the user.
   Covered by `take_fills_the_kit_shown_and_a_potion_goes_to_the_selection`.
8. Click "<" or ">" on "Kit of".
   *You will see* the next member's kit; the stores stay as they are. Press Esc.
   *You will see* the street, with the column at its usual place.

## Part 2: spells on the road
1. Select Durin, then Spells.
   *You will see* "Spells", "Caster < Durin >" and Close. One row per spell for the road (Guidance,
   Light, Bless, Cure Wounds), each with Cast and "N pt" after it. A spell on a member also reads "on
   Durin", the caster, while Durin is selected.
2. Select Brenna in the column.
   *You will see* "on Brenna" on the spells that target a member. The list stays Durin's.
3. Click Cast on Cure Wounds.
   *You will see* "Durin casts Cure Wounds (1 pt)" and "Brenna regains N hp, now H". The screen stays
   open and Durin's SP drop in the column.
4. Click Cast on Light.
   *You will see* "Light lights the party's way".
   Covered by `tests/cast.rs::the_caster_s_road_spells_cost_what_the_view_says_and_cast_as_shown`,
   `a_heal_goes_to_the_selection_or_the_caster_and_points_dim_the_costed` and
   `a_cast_from_the_column_keeps_the_screen_open_and_reports`.
5. Through MCP, `sim_command` with `{"Dev": {"SetSpellPoints": {"member": <Durin's id>, "points": 0}}}`.
   *You will see* Bless and Cure Wounds dim, "need N pt". The cantrips stay live.
6. Select Pip, then look at Spells under Pip.
   *You will see* Spells dim, "No spells for the road", if Pip knows none. Pressing Inventory while
   the spells screen is open switches to the inventory.
   Covered by `tests/roster.rs::each_screen_is_dim_with_its_reason_until_it_can_open` and
   `tests/cast.rs::a_member_with_no_road_spell_says_so_and_so_does_a_party_with_none`.

## Part 3: the message line
1. Close any screen. Turn right (E) to face north and press Shift+Space, or open Actions and choose
   Interact.
   *You will see* "There is nothing to do here" centred at the bottom, between the movement pad and
   the right-hand buttons.
2. Step forward.
   *You will see* the line go: a bare move clears it.
3. Open the pause (Esc).
   *You will see* no message line under a screen; it shows only while exploring with no screen up.
   A refusal shows in the alert colour. In play none reaches the line, since every button is lit by
   a trial. Covered by `tests/message.rs::the_line_shows_the_last_event_a_refusal_in_alert_and_clears_on_a_move`.

## Part 4: in a fight
1. Walk out of town until a fight starts (or through MCP, as in the party column's script). Select a
   member, then Inventory.
   *You will see* the rows with no buttons and "In a fight: look here, act from the fight's actions".
   Spells shows "In a fight: look here, cast from the fight's actions". Esc closes it.
   Covered by `tests/inventory.rs::in_a_fight_the_inventory_only_looks`.
