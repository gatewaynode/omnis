# Acceptance: the 3D preview from the cursor (M5.7)

The seventh step of M5 Editor v1 (`tasks/plans/m5-editor-v1.md` row 7, §Client side "Preview"): the editor's
right column shows the edited map in the game's own lines, seen from the cursor's cell the way the cursor
faces, rebuilt when the map changes. Two commits: the scene built with no world, then the picture.

## What was built

### 1. A scene with no world (2026-10-24)
- **`scene::Occupancy`**: what the scene asks of a world about a map, the doors' state and what stands on a
  level. The game's world answers from its state (`SimWorld`); the editor's preview answers as the map is
  placed (`editor::preview::AsPlaced`: every door shut, every object in its placed state and hidden ones
  shown, every standing group in place). `Scene::build_from` builds from a `MapData` that need not be among
  the loaded packs, which is where the edited map lives; `Scene::build` is the game's wrapper over it.
- **Two engine readings** with no world (`api::placed_on_level`, `api::standing_as_placed`): the same views
  the game draws from, taken from the map alone. The engine's rules did not move; no op, no schema.
- **`editor::preview`**: `scene(data, map, level)`, `pose(cursor, facing)` (the game's pose for that cell),
  `look(scene, pose)` (the eye over the ground, the yaw), `SIGHT` of 8 cells for the preview's fog.
- The renderer's line colours and fog fade are shared (`render::colour`, `render::fade`), so the picture
  will match the view line for line.

### 2. The picture (2026-10-24)
- **A second camera** (`shell/preview.rs`) on render layer 2 draws the preview's scene into an 800 by 450
  image with the view's own HDR, bloom, field of view, line width and depth bias; it is on while the editor
  is open and never draws the game's layer or the UI. Its lines are the view's (`render::colour`), faded over
  a sight of 8 cells; its fills the same black mesh; the crests the same.
- **The picture** sits at the top of the editor's right column, 376 by 212 pixels with a dim border, over the
  inspector, which keeps its own node so its rebuilds never touch it. Headless (no renderer) there is no
  picture and nothing else changes.
- **What it follows.** The scene is rebuilt when the map's resolve changes (`Editor.resolved_at`) or the level
  does, not on every cursor move; the camera moves with the cursor and turns with the facing every frame
  (Q and E, Face left and right). While the map does not resolve, the last good scene stays and the inspector
  says why.

## Steps
1. `cargo test -p omnis-vector --features test-fixtures --test integration editor_preview`.
   *You will see* the town's preview scene equal the game's scene of a fresh world (every door shut), a
   painted door appear in it, the yard's objects (hidden ones too), its pile and its standing rat drawn, and
   the pose at the cursor the game's own.

2. `just run --windowed --pack packs/base --pack /tmp/omnis-scratch`, Editor, open the town.
   *You will see* at the top of the right column a picture of the town from the start, facing west: the
   street, the buildings' walls and doors in the view's lines, the gate's portal mark on the floor if in sight.
3. Click cells on the plan; press Q and E.
   *You will see* the picture move to each cell and turn with the facing, at once.
4. Paint a wall across the street ahead of the cursor; paint a door in it; raise a cell's height.
   *You will see* each change stand in the picture as the game would draw it: the wall, the door's frame with
   its cross (shut), the cell's floor higher. Undo takes them away. Step the Level choice on the tower
   (`crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/levels`): the picture shows that level.
5. Make the map invalid (a ramp between heights more than 16 apart).
   *You will see* the inspector list the error, Save dim, and the picture keep the last good map until Undo.

Covered by `tests/editor_preview.rs` (3), the engine's `a_map_s_objects_and_groups_read_as_placed_with_no_world`
and `tests/geometry.rs`'s flat-map regression after the trait. The picture itself needs a renderer, as the
view does: the headless tests prove the scene it draws, the window is the owner's.

## Fixed after the first hand test (2026-10-24)
- **The picture was empty.** The preview's systems sat in the shell's draw set, which runs only while a game
  session exists; in the editor none ran, and the camera never switched on. They now run after the screens
  on their own condition (the editor open), and the plugin runs headless too (no camera, the scene kept),
  so a test adds it to the shell and reads the scene it would draw, rebuilt on a paint and not on a cursor
  move. Two labels drew a box for a glyph the font lacks ("Level…", "0‰"): now "Level..." and "per mille".
- **WASD** was reported broken; a test with Bevy's focus and text-input plugins on moves the cursor with
  A, S and the arrows. Not reproduced here; the owner's conditions are asked for.

## Known and expected
- The preview looks from the cursor's cell at the view's eye height, pitch level; there is no walking in it
  (Playtest, M5.8, is the walk).
- Hidden objects are drawn in the preview (an author sees everything); the game hides them until found.
- Every door is drawn shut.
