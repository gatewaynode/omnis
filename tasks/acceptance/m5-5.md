# Acceptance: brushes and painting (M5.5)

The fifth step of M5 Editor v1 (`tasks/plans/m5-editor-v1.md` §Client side "Input"): the plan is painted on.
No preview yet (M5.7), no Playtest (M5.8), no placement forms (M5.6).

**A Save writes the pack on disk and reseals it.** Hand-test on a scratch copy, never on `packs/test` itself:
`cp -R packs/test /tmp/omnis-scratch` and name `/tmp/omnis-scratch` as the last pack.

## What was built
- **Brushes** (`editor/brush.rs`): the palette is the open map's terrains in their order, then Wall, Door, Ramp,
  Open edge, Height and No ground. The tools column lists them two to a row, the current one marked `>`, the
  first nine numbered for the digit keys; the Height row carries the eighths choice (−64 to 64, arrows, `-`/`=`).
- **Painting.** A left click on the plan paints the brush at the cell under the pointer, or at the edge when the
  pointer is within a quarter of the cell's side of that border; the cursor goes to the cell. A drag is one
  stroke: one Undo takes it all back. Enter paints at the cursor; an edge brush then paints the side the cursor
  faces (Q/E turn it). A stair terrain rises the way the cursor faces. A refusal (a boundary edge, a height out
  of range) is said in the tools column and changes nothing.
- **Pick.** A right click takes what is under the pointer as the brush (the edge's kind, the cell's terrain or
  No ground) and the cell's height, and moves the cursor there without painting: it is also the way to inspect
  a cell without touching it.
- **History.** Undo and Redo buttons (dim with "nothing to undo/redo"), Cmd/Ctrl+Z, Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Z and
  Cmd/Ctrl+Y. The title shows `*` while the map differs from its file.
- **Save and Revert.** Save (button, Cmd/Ctrl+S, and on the Esc menu) is dim with "N errors: <the first>"
  while the loader would refuse the map; otherwise it writes the file in the canonical layout, reseals the pack
  (the patch version rises when the content changed) and reloads the packs from disk, so a new game walks the
  saved map. Revert reopens the file (dim with "no changes"); the cursor, level and zoom stay. Open says
  "Open (drops changes)" while dirty; the menu's Back to title says "Discard changes and leave".
- **The socket.** `editor_paint`, `editor_undo` and the rest act on the editor you see; the layer follows.

## Steps (in the window)
1. `cp -R packs/test /tmp/omnis-scratch`, then
   `just run --windowed --pack packs/base --pack /tmp/omnis-scratch`. Click **Editor**, step the Map choice to
   `test:map:town`, click **Open**.
   *You will see* the town from above and, in the tools column under the facing row, the brush rows: the
   town's terrains numbered 1 onward, the first marked `>`, then Wall, Door, Ramp, Open edge, Height with its
   eighths choice at 0, No ground; then Undo and Redo (dim), Save and Revert (Revert dim), Menu.
2. Click the brush **No ground** and click a grass cell inside the town.
   *You will see* the cell go dark, the cursor square on it, "No ground" in the inspector, and `*` after the
   root in the title. Undo is live.
3. Press the mouse on a cell and drag across four more, then release.
   *You will see* each cell darken as the pointer crosses it. Click **Undo** once: all of them come back.
   **Redo** brings them all back again.
4. Press **1**, then click the dark cells: the terrain returns. Press **Cmd+Z** twice.
5. Click **Wall** (or its digit) and click just inside a cell's eastern border.
   *You will see* a green wall line on that edge and "E wall" in the inspector. Click the same place with
   **Door**: an orange door. **Open edge** clears it. Click near the map's outer edge with Door: nothing changes
   and the tools column says a boundary edge is always a wall.
6. Click **Height**, press **=** eight times (the choice reads 8), move the cursor with the arrows to a cell and
   press **Enter**.
   *You will see* the cell brighten and "Height 8 eighths" in the inspector. Press **-** and Enter again: 7.
7. Right-click a plain grass cell.
   *You will see* the brush mark `>` move to the grass terrain and the eighths choice return to 0, the cursor on
   that cell, nothing painted. Right-click a wall edge: the mark moves to Wall.
8. Click **Ramp** and click an interior edge between two flat cells; then Height, `=` to 24, Enter on one side.
   *You will see* the inspector list "1 error" with the loader's words ("… ramp joins heights 24 and 0, more
   than 16 apart"), and Save dim with the same. Press Esc: the menu's Save is dim too. Resume.
9. Click **Undo** (the height), then **Save**.
   *You will see* "Saved data/maps/town.ron; test sealed at 0.1.x" in the tools column, the `*` gone, Revert dim.
   `/tmp/omnis-scratch/pack.ron` has the raised version and `data/maps/town.ron` the canonical layout.
10. Esc, **Back to title**, **New game**, create a party, and walk to the ramp.
    *You will see* the ramp you painted (the pack was reloaded with the saved map).
11. Esc, Back to title, Editor, Open the town again. Paint a cell No ground. Press Esc.
    *You will see* "Discard changes and leave" where "Back to title" was. Resume, click **Revert**: the cell is
    grass again, the `*` gone, the cursor where it was.
12. With the window open, call the MCP tool `editor_paint` (level 0, x 2, y 2, brush `{"tile": {"glyph": "."}}`
    or any glyph the inspector shows), then `editor_undo`.
    *You will see* the plan and the title change with each.

Covered by `crates/omnis-vector/tests/editor_paint.rs` (the click, the drag as one step, the edge band, the
edge brushes and the boundary refusal, the digits, the height by key and arrow, Enter at the cursor, the right
click's pick, Save refused in the loader's words and nothing written, Save writing, resealing and reloading,
Cmd+S, the menu's Save, Revert, the menu's leave label, Cmd+Z and Cmd+Shift+Z), the `brush.rs` unit tests (the
palette, the brush from a target) and the M5.4 tests, which now use `tests/common/editor.rs`.

## Known and expected
- The loader's full check runs after every paint, so a drag across a very large map may lag; the status is
  cached between paints, not asked for each frame.
- No hover highlight of the target yet; the inspector follows the cursor, which follows the paint.
- Open while dirty drops the changes; the label says so, there is no confirmation (the plan's "never
  destructive" is the pick's rule, not Open's).
- The same content saved again keeps its version: the seal is of the content.

## Covered throughout
- Gate VERIFY-GREEN; Sentrux rules pass.
- `SAVE_SCHEMA`, `PROTOCOL`, the op count and the replays unchanged: nothing of the engine moved.
