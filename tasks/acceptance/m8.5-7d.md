# Acceptance: the character save and the world folder (M8.5 7d, reworked)

This is the script for accepting M8.5 sub-step 7d (`tasks/plans/m8.5-save-model.md`, amended 2026-10-17). A save
is no longer the whole world (PRD D28). The owner rejected the first 7d, which replayed every command on load:
loads of 4–5 s are normal for the genre, and moving changes nothing in the world but time. So:
- **The quick save `.omnis/quick.ron` holds the party as it stands** and the **journal of interactions**: each
  command that changed what the world holds (a door, an object, a pile, a cleared encounter, a world flag, a
  region's time), with the changes it made.
- **Moving, turning, resting, buying and spotting things journal nothing.** A hidden lever you find is the party's
  knowledge now, not the map's. **Entering another region is one entry:** that region's time catches up with the
  party (PRD §7.8), and the region's clock is the world's.
- **The world's bases sit in a world folder**, `.omnis/worlds/<id>/`: `manifest.ron`, `world/<hash>.ron` and
  `regions/<pack>/<name>/<hash>.ron`, each named by the hash of its text.
- **Loading** sets the party down and lays the journal's changes over the bases. No rule runs, so a rule changed
  since (the dev socket's `rules.set`) does not stop a save from loading.
- **An older save** (schema 7 and before, a whole world) loads by becoming the base of an empty journal.
  `SAVE_SCHEMA` is 8.

**Measured** (release, the 7a wander):

| Commands | Interactions | Journal | Save | Load |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10,000 | 34 | 7 KB | 94 KB | 0.10 s |
| 50,000 | 166 | 49 KB | 158 KB | 0.30 s |

The first 7d needed 120 KB and 0.2 s for 10,000 commands, and 605 KB and 1.8 s for 50,000. Most of the save now
is the party itself (the automap); the journal grows with interactions, not with walking.

**Start.** First copy your current quick save aside: `cp .omnis/quick.ron .omnis/quick-v7.ron` (the first new
save overwrites it). Then run `just run --windowed --pack packs/base --pack packs/test --pack
crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/objects`, start a new game with devtools on, and make a party.

## Steps
1. Walk around inside the town for a minute, without leaving it, then Esc, Debug, the World page.
   *You will see* "Journal: 0 interactions since the world's base". Esc and **Save**: "Saved to .omnis/quick.ron".
   `ls -R .omnis/worlds` shows a folder with a sixteen-letter name holding `manifest.ron`.
2. Walk out of the town into the meadow and look at the World page again.
   *You will see* "Journal: 1 interaction since the world's base": the meadow's time caught up with the party.
   Walk on in the meadow: the count stays 1, unless a fight leaves loot on the ground (a pile is the world's).
3. On the World page, teleport to `objects:map:yard`, cell (0, 2), facing North (another region: 2). The lever at
   (0, 0) is hidden (difficulty 12). If no "noticed" line appears, step to (0, 1) and **Search** until it is found.
   *You will see* the lever drawn, and the World page still says 2 interactions: finding it is the party's.
4. Standing at (0, 1) facing North, **Use** the lever ("pulled"). Teleport to (3, 2) facing South and **Use** the
   gate: it opens.
   *You will see* "Journal: 4 interactions since the world's base".
5. Esc, **Save**, then quit to the title and **Load**.
   *You will see* "Loaded .omnis/quick.ron: 4 interactions applied in T ms". The party stands where it stood, the
   gate is open, the lever is down and still drawn (found).
6. Mid-fight: teleport back to the meadow, walk until something attacks, take a turn, Save, then Load.
   *You will see* the fight resume on the same turn.
7. An old save. Quit, then run `cp crates/omnis-sim/tests/saves/v7.ron .omnis/quick.ron`. The fixture was made on
   the base and test packs only, so start the game **without** the objects pack: `just run --windowed --pack
   packs/base --pack packs/test`. Load from the title.
   *You will see* "Loaded .omnis/quick.ron: converted from a schema 7 save", and the party at the dungeon's first
   door, the door open. Save again: `ls -R .omnis/worlds` shows a second world folder with `regions/…/<hash>.ron`
   files, the old world made a base.

**Your own old quick save will be refused**, with "save was made with different packs". It was made with the
objects pack before 7c changed it. The window never forces a load, so such a save waits for 7h, which loads
across pack changes. Keep the copy.

## What the tests cover
- **`omnis-sim/tests/journal.rs`:**
  - a step, a turn and a purse journal nothing;
  - entering another region is one entry, with that region's clock;
  - pulling the lever is one entry with the world flag and the lever's state;
  - a party flag journals nothing and a world flag one change;
  - a refused command leaves the journal as it was;
  - the journal is not in the fingerprint;
  - **after every command** of both golden replays and the yard's lever and gate (opened, then shut back to as
    placed), the journal laid over the start holds exactly what the world holds.
- **`omnis-sim/tests/disk.rs`:**
  - a new game's save names no bases, and making a party journals nothing;
  - both goldens save and load after every command, as do an inn and mid-fight;
  - 200 moves journal nothing and a party placed by hand saves as it stands;
  - a save loads after `rules.set` changed the random-encounter rule;
  - a change the play never made, a wrong fingerprint and a world name that climbs out of the folder are refused;
  - other packs are refused unless forced;
  - the v7 fixture converts, and an altered or missing base is refused;
  - v1 to v7 all convert;
  - the save rule holds.
- **`omnis-sim/tests/notice.rs`:** spotting the lever leaves the map's state unchanged, and an older save that
  kept finds on the map gives them to the party.
- **Mutations:** 17 of 17 caught, covering:
  - the empty-entry rule (both ways) and pruning after a command, of maps and of objects;
  - party and world flags, contacts and streams journaled or not;
  - the door, value and removal diffs;
  - the set marker;
  - changes laid on load;
  - the checkpoint taken now;
  - the legacy finds;
  - the fingerprint.
