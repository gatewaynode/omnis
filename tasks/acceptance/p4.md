# Acceptance: town services (parity P4)

The script for your acceptance of parity step P4 (`tasks/plans/p4.md`). The town's seven services
now open their panel in the vector client, in place of the V2 notice. Every offer, price and
refusal is the engine's (`service_view`, the same view MCP's `service_get` returns); the client
only words and groups them. By your decisions of 2026-10-11 there is no tool bar and no step
confirmation: walking in enters at once, and Leave leaves at once.

The town (`test:map:town`): the street runs along y 2 from the start at (10, 2), facing West.
North of it are the inn (1, 1), the temple (4, 1), the trainer (7, 1) and the guild (10, 1). South
of it are the smith (1, 3), the tavern (4, 3) and the bank (7, 3).

## Part 1: in and out of the guild
1. `just run --windowed --seed 1`. You start in the town with the fixed party (Brenna, Durin,
   Ilvara, Pip). Turn right (E) to face north, open the door if it is shut (Shift+Space runs the
   square's default action), and step forward (W).
   *You will see* "Mages' guild" over the panel, at once, with no question. The first row reads
   "Gold 60 gp 0 sp 0 cp  Bank 0 gp 0 sp 0 cp  Food 40" with Leave beside it. Under "Spells":
   "Ilvara: Shatter" and "Ilvara: Acid Arrow", each with a dim "Learn: level 2 spell, up to level
   1 now". The HUD line reads "The party enters the Mages' guild". The party column is hidden
   while the panel is up.
2. Click Leave.
   *You will see* the street again, with no question, and "The party leaves the Mages' guild".
   Covered by `tests/service.rs::walking_into_the_guild_opens_its_panel_and_leave_steps_back_out`.
3. Walk back in, then press Esc.
   *You will see* the street, not the pause. The pause is on the Menu button.
   Covered by `escape_in_a_service_leaves_rather_than_pausing` and
   `escape_in_the_shell_leaves_the_service`.

## Part 2: the smith
1. Walk west along the street to (1, 2), face south, and step into the smithy.
   *You will see* two panels. "For sale" lists Dagger, Handaxe, Mace and the rest, each with Buy
   and its price in dim small type. "Your stores" lists what the party carries, each with Sell
   and "pays X", or "Nothing to offer" when the stores are empty.
2. Click Buy on the Dagger.
   *You will see* the gold go down by 2 gp, "Bought 1 Dagger for 2 gp 0 sp 0 cp" under the
   panels and as the HUD's newest line, and "Dagger x1" (or more) under "Your stores".
3. Scroll the mouse wheel over "For sale".
   *You will see* the list scroll. The panel stays between the HUD's lines and the corner
   buttons, at 1280×720 too.
4. Sell the dagger, then Leave.
   *You will see* "Sold 1 Dagger for 1 gp 0 sp 0 cp", then the street. The message under the
   panel goes with it.
   Covered by `a_purchase_reports_on_the_panel_and_the_report_goes_with_it`,
   `the_wheel_scrolls_the_panel_under_the_cursor` and
   `the_smith_s_rows_name_the_item_its_price_and_why_not`.

## Part 3: the bank
1. Step into the bank at (7, 3).
   *You will see* the money line, Leave, and a "Gold pieces" field with Deposit and Withdraw, both
   dim: "Type an amount".
2. Type `5x`.
   *You will see* only `5`: the field takes digits only. Deposit is live; Withdraw reads "the bank
   holds 0 gp 0 sp 0 cp".
3. Click Deposit.
   *You will see* "Deposited 5 gp 0 sp 0 cp", and the money line move 5 gp from Gold to Bank.
   Withdraw is now live. Click it and the 5 gp come back.
4. Type more gold than the party has (say `999`).
   *You will see* Deposit dim, "not enough money". Type `123456789` and the field keeps 8 digits.
   No more than 42,949,672 gold is taken, because the engine counts the bank in copper.
5. Leave and step back in.
   *You will see* the field empty again.
   Covered by `the_bank_takes_whole_gold_sends_copper_and_says_why_not` and
   `the_outcome_clears_when_the_screen_changes_and_the_amount_on_entering`.

## Part 4: the inn, the tavern, the temple, the trainer
1. The tavern (4, 3).
   *You will see* "On offer": "A rumor" (Listen, free) and "Food for 1 day" (Buy, 0 gp 5 sp 0
   cp). Listen puts the rumor in quotes on the panel; Buy adds a day of food to the money line.
2. The inn (1, 1).
   *You will see* "A night's rest" with "Take a room" and its price. Take it; take it again, and
   the button is dim: "rested too recently (Hh MMm)".
3. The temple (4, 1).
   *You will see* "On offer" with three rows per member: "Brenna, 12 of 12 HP" (Heal), "Brenna,
   no conditions" (Cure), "Brenna, alive" (Raise), each dim with its reason while there is
   nothing to treat. "Spells" lists Durin's spells to learn, with prices. The list scrolls.
4. The trainer (7, 1).
   *You will see* "Levels": "Brenna, level 1 to 2" and so on, each dim "0 of 300 XP"; "Spell
   picks": "Nothing to offer".
5. Through MCP, `party_get` for Brenna's `id`, then `sim_command` with `{"Dev": {"SetXp":
   {"member": <id>, "xp": 300}}}`. Leave and step back in.
   *You will see* Train live with its price. Click it: "Brenna reaches level 2: +N HP, …".
   Covered by `each_service_opens_with_its_money_leave_and_lists_in_the_other_client_s_order`,
   `every_offer_sends_what_the_view_promised_and_nothing_it_refuses` and
   `the_temple_names_what_it_treats_and_the_trainer_the_next_level`.
