# Acceptance: the world in its parts (M8.5 7b)

This is the script for accepting M8.5 sub-step 7b (`tasks/plans/m8.5-save-model.md`), the second piece of the
save model (PRD D28). The engine can now cut a world into the parts the save model keeps apart, and put them back
together:
- the **manifest**: the seed and the packs;
- the **world base**: what belongs to no region;
- one **region base** per region play has changed;
- the party's **checkpoint**.

Putting them back must give the same world, to the fingerprint. Nothing is written to disk yet (that is 7d), and
saves are unchanged.

**What goes where:**
- **A map's state** goes to its region, including a map you only walked through (it is in the fingerprint).
- **Clocks and contacts** go to their holder's part: the party's to the checkpoint, a region's to its base.
  When the party meets a region, each side keeps its own memory of the meeting.
- **Random streams:** the stream a region shares with the party is the region's. The party's own (combat,
  rests, encounters) are the checkpoint's.
- **Flags** all sit in the world base until 7c lets a pack say which flags are the party's.
- **A region nothing has changed has no base.** That is what "no file" will mean in 7d, so a new game is its
  checkpoint alone: base 0 is the packs.

**Choices of mine:**
- **Split is a button, not a live line.** The report writes out the whole world, which is too much to do every
  frame. It is a report only and sends nothing to the engine.
- **The lines go away once the world moves on** (one command later), so an old report is never shown as current.
- **A catch-all.** Holders that are neither the party nor a region (actors, separated characters, projects:
  none exist yet) and the state of a map no loaded pack has go to the world base, so the round trip holds when
  M9 and M10 add them.

**Start.** Run `just run --windowed --pack packs/base --pack packs/test --pack crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/objects`,
then start a new game with devtools on and make a party.

## Steps
1. Press Esc, then Debug, then the World page, then the **Split** button under the journal lines.
   *You will see*:
   - "World base: N bytes; checkpoint: N bytes";
   - "No region has changed: each is as its pack wrote it", or one line for the town if the start touched it;
   - "Round trip: ok".
2. Close the panel and turn once. Reopen the World page.
   *You will see* the split lines gone, because the world has moved on. Press Split again to see them.
3. Walk out of town through the gate into the meadow and open a door on the way. Press Split.
   *You will see* a line per changed region, such as "test:region:…: N bytes, 1 map", and "Round trip: ok".
4. Fight something (the meadow's encounters, or teleport near the rats in the dungeon), and press Split
   mid-fight.
   *You will see* "Round trip: ok": a fight splits and joins like anything else.
5. Teleport to `objects:map:yard`, move or open a crate, and press Split.
   *You will see* the yard's region among the lines, its bytes larger after the crate.

## What the tests cover
- `omnis-sim/tests/split.rs`:
  - a new world splits into the checkpoint alone and joins back to itself;
  - both goldens round-trip after every command, through explore, encounter and combat (the fight's own bus
    subscription included);
  - three services round-trip;
  - each part holds its own (the region's map, clock, side of the contact and shared stream; the party's side
    and streams);
  - an empty map state keeps its region base;
  - the catch-alls go to the world base;
  - `join` refuses a duplicate, a party nowhere, and other packs (unless forced);
  - the report sizes the parts, and says when they do not join.
- `omnis-cli/tests/wander.rs`: a 2,000-command wander round-trips every 25 commands, through deaths, revivals,
  rests, teleports and fights on every map.
- `omnis-vector/tests/debug.rs`: Split shows the lines and "Round trip: ok", and hides them once the world
  moves.
