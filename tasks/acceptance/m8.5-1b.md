# Acceptance: levels in one map (M8.5 step 1b)

This is the script for accepting M8.5 step 1b (`tasks/plans/m8.5-levels.md`), built to your answers of
2026-10-12:
- **Levels in one map:** a map file holds levels above and below its own; a position carries its level.
- **Joins:** stairs (two-way, by a step), ladders and shafts (climbed with an action), holes (a one-way drop).
- **Overlaps:** the levels just above and below are drawn dimmer at their real heights. The rules stay per level.
- **Test data:** a new fixture, the tower (`crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/levels`). The shipped maps are unchanged.
- **Level names** are text keys, as every other name you see.

Departures from the plan: no ground is written `_` (the test dungeon uses `.` as its floor); the save format did
not change (version 7), so old saves load and the replays did not move; the tower's hole sits beside the bridge
at (5, 2), so the bridge can be crossed.

## The tower
Seven by five, four levels:
- **Cellar (−1):** under the tower only, with the ladder's foot at (1, 1).
- **Ground (0):** grass, a road along row 2, the tower at x 1–2, y 0–1 (its door on the south side of (2, 1)),
  a stair up at (1, 0), the ladder at (1, 1), and a stair up in the courtyard at (4, 3).
- **Upper floor (1):** the tower's floor with a stair down at (1, 0) and the ladder; a bridge east from the tower
  and south over the road (column 4, rows 1–3), a hole beside it over the road at (5, 2), and the bridge's stair
  down at (4, 3).
- **Roof (2):** the tower's top, where the ladder ends.

## Part 1: in the window
1. Run `just run --windowed --pack packs/base --pack packs/test --pack crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/levels`.
   Start a new game. Open the debug panel (Esc, then Debug, the "World" page), pick `levels:map:tower`, set x 6,
   y 2, Level "Test Tower (0)", facing West, press Go, and close the panel.
   *You will see* the road running west, the bridge crossing it overhead in dimmer lines, the red hole beside it,
   and the yellow ladder rising through the tower. The HUD's first line reads `levels:map:tower  Test Tower (0)
   cell (6, 2) facing West`, and "Test Tower (0)" stands left of the minimap.
2. Walk west to (4, 2), under the bridge, and look up (mouse look or PageUp).
   *You will see* the bridge's floor and railing above you, and the hole's outline beside it.
3. Walk to (4, 4), turn north, and step onto the courtyard stair at (4, 3).
   *You will see* yellow treads climbing the cell, then the view rise onto the bridge: the event lines read
   "Moved to (4, 3) facing North" and "Moved to (4, 3) on Upper floor (1) facing North". The HUD and the caption
   now say Upper floor (1), the road is drawn dimmer below, and the minimap shows the upper floor only.
4. Step off the stair north onto the bridge, then back south onto it.
   *You will see* nothing on the step off; stepping back onto the stair takes you down to the courtyard.
5. Go back up, walk north to (4, 2), and turn east toward the hole at (5, 2). Step onto it.
   *You will see* the view drop to the road below: "Moved to (5, 2) on Test Tower (0) facing East". There is no
   way back up from the road there.
6. Walk into the tower through its door (from the road at (2, 2), north to (2, 1)) and step west onto the ladder
   at (1, 1). Press Space.
   *You will see* "Climb up" and "Climb down" in the Actions menu. Choose Climb down: you are in the cellar, the
   ground drawn dimmer above you.
7. Press Space and Climb up three times.
   *You will see* the ground, the upper floor, then the roof. On the roof, Space offers only "Climb down".
8. Climb down to the upper floor, step east to (2, 1) and north to (2, 0), turn west, and step onto the stair
   down at (1, 0).
   *You will see* yourself back on the ground at (1, 0); stepping off and back on the ground's stair takes you up.
9. On the upper floor, walk to the bridge at (4, 1) and try to step north.
   *You will see* "Blocked: no ground".
10. Quit, and run `just run --windowed` with no packs named.
    *You will see* the town, the meadow and the dungeon play as before, with no level in the HUD and no caption by
    the minimap.

  - `tests/scene.rs`: neighbours at their heights and dimmer, each join's marks, no ceiling under the level above,

## Known and expected
- **Colours are provisional** until step 5: stairs and ladders pale gold, holes red, neighbours at a fifth of
  their brightness.
- **A stair's treads always climb north to south** in the drawing; the map format has no direction for them yet.
- **Falling** through a hole does no damage (X11 keeps falling for later).
- **The mouse-look view** may see the neighbouring levels through floors: there are no solid fills until step 3.
