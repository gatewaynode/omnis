# Acceptance: groups that stand in view (M8.5 step 2c)

This is the script for accepting M8.5 step 2c (`tasks/plans/m8.5-objects.md`, "2c — visible beings"): a fixed
encounter that stands where you can see it, drawn as a figure, met by walking in as before or by Use from the
next cell. Built in three items:
1. **Data:** a placed group may `stand` at an offset in its cell; a monster's figure is its own or one by its
   size; the yard gains two pack rats at (5, 1), facing West, met once.
2. **Engine:** the viewport lists the standing groups; `Approach` meets one in reach without moving the party,
   and Run puts it back where it stood; Use lists a group after the door and before the objects.
3. **Client:** the figure (a body and a head, orange for now); Use and the verbs offer Approach; a search names
   the group.

**Your answers (2026-10-17):** one figure per group; a figure blocks no step, so walking in meets it as before;
Use lists it among the choices.

**Departures and choices of mine** (each in the plan's "Decided by me" section):
- A standing group has an id (`rat`), which Approach names. Groups that do not stand keep none until step 7.
- A figure blocks neither movement nor sight.
- Approach takes no time, and closes the verbs screen, as the encounter takes over.
- The figure's colour is provisional until step 5's colour roles.
- Use's label for a lone group is "Approach: Pack rat x2". Names are not lowercased, since some monsters
  have proper names.

**Start.** Run `just run --windowed --pack packs/base --pack packs/test --pack crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/objects`,
start a new game with devtools on, make a party, and teleport to `objects:map:yard` (Esc, Debug, the World
page). Each "stand at" below is a teleport there, or a walk. Watch the overlay's "refusals 0  disagreements 0"
throughout.

## Part 1: seeing them
1. Stand at (2, 1) facing East.
   *You will see* an orange figure at (5, 1), a little east of the cell's middle, under the ladder: a body
   narrowing to the shoulders, with a small head. Turn West: it is still drawn behind you when you look round
   with the mouse, as objects are.
2. Climb the ladder at (6, 1) and look down from the loft.
   *You will see* the figure below, dimmed with the ground level.

## Part 2: meeting them from the next cell
3. Stand at (5, 2) facing North and press Space.
   *You will see* "Approach: Pack rat x2" first in the actions, then Search here.
4. Click "Approach: Pack rat x2".
   *You will see* the encounter's choices, two pack rats. The figure is no longer drawn behind the panel.
   You have not moved: the overlay still says (5, 2).
5. Choose Run (or Hide), until one works.
   *You will see* the figure standing again at (5, 1). After Run the party is at (5, 2) facing South; after
   Hide it stays as it was, facing North.

## Part 3: choosing among things in reach
6. Stand at (4, 1) facing East and press Space, then Use....
   *You will see* "Within reach": "Pack rat  x2" with Approach first, then the plaque with its verbs.
7. Press Search here instead (Space, Search here), then read the notice.
   *You will see*, after the line with the search's roll, a line starting "Within reach: Pack rat x2".
8. Press Space, Use..., then Approach.
   *You will see* the verbs close and the encounter open.

## Part 4: beating them
9. Fight, and win.
   *You will see* the fight's end, any loot the rats drop lying where you stood, and no figure at (5, 1)
   afterwards.
10. Walk into (5, 1) and back out.
    *You will see* nothing met: the group was met once and is beaten for good.

## Part 5: walking in, as before
11. Start a new game, teleport to the yard at (4, 1) facing East, and step East into (5, 1).
    *You will see* the encounter begin as any placed group's does. Run puts you back at (4, 1), facing West.

**Accepted when** each part reads as written, refusals and disagreements stay 0, and the figure reads as a
creature, not an object. Tell me if its shape or colour should change; step 5 settles the colours.
