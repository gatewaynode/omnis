# Acceptance: the debug panel and the settings (parity P8)

This is the script for accepting parity step P8 (`tasks/plans/p8.md`). It covers two screens, built to your answers of 2026-10-11:
- **Settings:** reduced motion, the floor grid, one Quality preset, the font and the interface scale. Changes wait for Apply. The screen opens from the title and the pause.
- **The debug panel:** four pages, opened from the pause's Debug or with backtick.

The fixed party is Brenna (fighter), Durin (cleric), Ilvara (wizard) and Pip (rogue). The game starts in the town at (10, 2), facing West.

Your settings are kept in `.omnis/settings.ron`. To start with the defaults, move the file aside first.

## Part 1: the settings
1. Run `just run --windowed`.
   *You will see* the title: "New game", "Load quick save", "Settings", then "Quit".
2. Click Settings.
   *You will see* "SETTINGS" with these rows:
   - "Reduced motion < off >";
   - "Floor grid < shown >";
   - "Quality < high >";
   - "Font < built-in >";
   - "Interface scale < 100% >";
   - "Apply: nothing changed" (dim) and "Close".

   Covered by `tests/settings.rs::apply_is_dim_until_a_setting_changes_and_then_sends_the_draft`.
3. Click ">" on Font, then Close, then Settings again.
   *You will see* the font unchanged and the screen back at "built-in": Close dropped the change. Esc does the same.
   Covered by `close_and_esc_drop_the_draft`.
4. Set Font to Inter and click Apply.
   *You will see* "Settings saved to .omnis/settings.ron" under the buttons, and every text in Inter, the title's too, once you close.
   Covered by `apply_on_the_title_s_settings_keeps_them_and_writes_the_file` and `the_font_applied_dresses_every_text_and_text_that_comes_later`.
5. Start a new game. Press Esc, then click Settings on the pause.
   - Set "Floor grid < hidden >", "Quality < low >" and "Reduced motion < on >", then Apply and close both screens.
   - *You will see* no floor or ceiling grid, only walls, doors and terrain. The lines are thin, with no glow.
   - Press Q and E. *You will see* each quarter turn happen at once, with no swing. Walk and stop near a cell's edge: the view settles onto the cell at once.

   Covered by `the_hidden_grid_drops_the_floor_and_ceiling_and_keeps_the_rest`, `quality_sets_the_bloom_on_every_camera` and the unit tests in `shell/movement.rs`.
6. Set "Interface scale" to 200% and Apply.
   - In a 1600×900 window you will see "120% fits this window" after the scale. The interface grows to 120%: the HUD, its buttons, the party column and the screens.
   - On your 5120×1440 display the full 200% fits.
   - Walk into a fight. *You will see* the fight's panels, figures and party column grown alike, with the clicks still landing on the figures.

   Covered by `a_scale_larger_than_the_window_holds_says_how_far_it_fits`, `tests/combat.rs::at_a_larger_scale_the_fight_zooms_with_the_interface_and_a_click_still_finds_its_target` and `tests/arena.rs::the_fight_draws_at_the_default_scale_as_before_and_fits_at_half_and_double`.
7. Quit and run again.
   *You will see* the settings as you left them: the file is read at start.
8. Set everything back to the defaults if you wish, and Apply.

## Part 2: the debug panel
1. In a game, press Esc.
   *You will see* "Debug" lit beside "Settings". (A world without devtools shows it dim: "Devtools are off in this game".)
   Covered by `tests/debug.rs::the_pause_s_debug_is_dim_outside_a_devtools_world`.
2. Click Debug.
   *You will see* "DEBUG" with:
   - "Page < Member >" and "Member < Brenna >";
   - "Hit points [12]  Set  of 12", "Spell points", "Experience … level 1";
   - "Ability < STR > [score] Set";
   - "Condition < Blinded >  On / off  off";
   - "Close".
3. Type 5 into Hit points and click Set.
   *You will see* Brenna at 5/12 in yellow in the party column once the panel closes, and "dev: Brenna hp 5" among the HUD's lines.
   Covered by `the_member_page_reads_the_member_and_a_typed_number_is_sent_saturated` and `backtick_opens_the_panel_with_mouse_look_on_esc_closes_it_and_an_edit_logs_a_line`.
4. Switch Member to Durin, type 300 into Experience and click Set.
   *You will see* "level 2" after it.
   Covered by `the_member_switch_ability_and_condition_reach_the_chosen_member`.
5. Go to Page "Party". Pick Item "Spyglass", Count 1, and click To member, then To stores.
   *You will see* a spyglass in Durin's kit and one in the stores (the party column's Inventory). Set Copper to 100000.
   Covered by `the_party_page_sets_money_and_gives_items_to_the_member_or_the_stores`.
6. Go to Page "World".
   *You will see* "The packs declare no flags", then "Map < test:map:town (12 by 5) >", "x 10", "y 2", "Facing < west >" and "Go".
   - Step Map to the dungeon and click Go.
   - *You will see* the party in the dungeon at the cell shown, and "dev: teleport to … facing west".

   Covered by `the_world_page_teleports_with_the_cell_held_inside_the_map`.
7. Close the panel (Esc), walk into the rats and click Fight. Then press backtick.
   - *You will see* the panel over the fight. On Page "World", Go is dim with the engine's refusal.
   - Go to Page "Fight". *You will see* "Stack < Giant Rat (2 of 2) >", "Lead hit points [n] Set" and "Kill".
   - Click Kill. *You will see* the fight end, and "No fight is on" on the page.

   Covered by `in_a_fight_go_is_dim_with_the_engine_s_reason_and_the_fight_page_kills_a_stack`.
8. Press backtick again.
   *You will see* the panel close. With mouse look on, backtick frees the pointer as the panel opens.

## Not in this script
- Every lit button on every page sends a command the engine accepts: `every_lit_button_on_every_page_sends_a_command_the_engine_accepts`.
- The settings file read leniently:
  - an unknown key is ignored, a word out of the list falls back, and a scale out of range is clamped;
  - a malformed file or a link gives the defaults with a note;
  - covered by the four file tests in `tests/settings.rs`.
- The dev lines' words for every edit: `tests/labels.rs::dev_edits_read_as_lines_with_names`.
- A flag's Set: the shipped packs declare no flags, so it is not reachable.
