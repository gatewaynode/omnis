## Part 1: in the window
1. Run `just run --windowed --pack packs/base --pack packs/test --pack crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/heights`.
   Start a new game. Note the HUD's fps and ms in the town.
2. Open the debug panel (Esc, then Debug), go to the "World" page and step Map to `heights:map:hills`. Set x 0,
   y 0, facing East, and click Go. Close the panel.
   *You will see:*
   - the ground ahead rising gently (the slope 0, 2, 4);
   - beyond it, an amber hatched face up to the terrace at 12;
   - to the right, the long face of the causeway at 16 along row 2.
3. Walk east.
   *You will see:*
   - the camera rising with the slope, smoothly and without a jolt;
   - at the face, the party held, with "Blocked: too steep" once.

   The minimap shows the face as an amber edge.
4. Go to (0, 2) facing East, and walk east.
   *You will see:*
   - pale treads across the ramp;
   - the camera climbing two wall heights, onto the causeway with faces on both sides.

   The minimap shows a pale tick on the ramp's edge.
5. Go to (2, 4) facing East (the hill).
   *You will see:*
   - the camera two walls up;
   - the wall between x 3 and 4 below you, and the ground beyond it.
6. Go to (4, 3) facing North.
   *You will see* the causeway's face rising above the view.
7. Note the HUD's fps and ms here and compare them with the town's. Before this step, the hills drew at 145 fps
   (6.9 ms) in your window.
8. Quit, and run `just run --windowed` with no packs named.
   *You will see* the town, the meadow and the dungeon drawn exactly as before.
9. In Settings, hide the grid.
   *You will see* the floor grid go while cliffs and ramps stay.
