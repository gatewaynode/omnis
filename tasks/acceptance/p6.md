# Acceptance: camp and rest, the character sheet, tactics (parity P6)

The script for your acceptance of parity step P6 (`tasks/plans/p6.md`). A Camp button now stands at
the bottom right, live only while exploring; the party column's Sheet opens the character sheet; the
sheet's Tactics opens the member's declared reactions. The column stands beside all three. Every
button is lit by the engine: the rest view's refusal, or a trial of its exact command. In the engine,
each member's known spells now carry their point cost and action cost, and the fight's spell list
shows the cost the cast charges.

The fixed party: Brenna (fighter), Durin (cleric), Ilvara (wizard), Pip (rogue). The start is the town
at (10, 2), facing West: 60 gp, 40 food, nobody hurt.

## Part 1: camp
1. `just run --windowed --seed 1`.
   *You will see* "Camp  R" before "Actions  Space" and "Menu  Esc" at the bottom right, lit.
2. Through MCP, `party_get` for Brenna's `id`, then `sim_command` with `{"Dev": {"SetHp": {"member": <id>,
   "hp": 2}}}`. Click Camp (or press R).
   *You will see* "Camp" in the room left of the party column, which stays shown. The first row reads
   "The night eats 4 food; the stores hold 40" with Close. A row per member, "Brenna  HP 2/12  Hit dice
   1/1 d10" with a Spend number and "spend 0" after it; the others read "unhurt" and have no number.
   Short rest is dim: "Choose hit dice to spend". Long rest is lit.
   Covered by `tests/camp.rs::the_rows_read_the_rest_view_and_a_count_runs_to_what_may_be_spent`.
3. Press W.
   *You will see* nothing move: the party does not walk while the camp is open.
4. Click ">" on Brenna's Spend twice.
   *You will see* "spend 1": the count stops at what she may spend. Short rest lights.
5. Click Short rest.
   *You will see* the camp stay open, "Brenna regains N hp, now H" under it, and her row read "Hit dice
   0/1 d10" and "unhurt" or "no hit dice left". The column's HP for her goes up.
   Covered by `a_short_rest_spends_the_dice_chosen_and_heals` and
   `the_camp_button_is_live_exploring_and_opens_the_camp_beside_the_column`.
6. Click Long rest.
   *You will see* the rest's newest line under the camp (a member's healing; the HUD's recent lines
   also show "The party rests for the night and eats 4 food"), Long rest now dim "rested too recently
   (Nh MMm)", and the food line "the stores hold 36".
7. Through MCP, `{"Dev": {"SetFood": {"food": 0}}}`.
   *You will see* Long rest dim (still "rested too recently" first, as the engine checks that first).
   Covered by `a_long_rest_shows_its_refusal_too_soon_and_without_food`, which also checks "the rest eats
   N food; the stores hold 0".
8. Press Esc, then R, then R again.
   *You will see* the camp close, open and close.
9. Turn right (E) and step forward onto the guild.
   *You will see* the Camp button dim, and R does nothing: the room is the rest indoors. Leave.
   Covered by `the_camp_button_is_dim_in_a_service_and_in_a_fight` (in a fight too).

   *You will see* Magic: "Casting Wisdom  Points 2/2", "Spells", then Guidance, Light and Sacred Flame

   Covered by `the_magic_page_prices_each_known_spell_as_the_view_does_and_lists_effects` (with Light cast:

   *You will see* Gear: "Worn and wielded" and a row per slot (Main hand, Off hand, Ranged, Body), "-" where

## Part 3: tactics
No fixed member knows a reaction spell, so Ilvara learns Shield first.
1. Walk onto the guild (10, 1) and learn Shield for Ilvara (through MCP `{"Dev": {"SetGold": {"gold":
   100000}}}` (copper) if Learn is dim for money). Leave.
2. Open Ilvara's Sheet and click Tactics.
   *You will see* "Tactics" beside the column: "Member < Ilvara >", "Reactions < on >", Close; "Declared
   reactions, in the order they are considered", "None declared"; "New reaction", "Action < Shield >" and
   "on < attacked >", "When < all of these >" with Add condition, and Save, New.
3. Click Add condition, then set its Number to 50 (the slider, or the arrows).
   *You will see* a condition row: "If < hit points % >", "Who < me >", "Is < < >", the Number, Drop.
4. Click Save.
   *You will see* "Ilvara's tactics are set" and the row "1. Shield on attacked, when me HP < 50%"; the
   form starts over.
   Covered by `tests/tactics.rs::save_declares_what_the_form_reads_and_edit_writes_over_it`.
5. Click Edit on the row, set the Number to 25, click "When" to "any of these", and Save.
   *You will see* "Editing reaction 1" while editing, then still one row, now "... < 25%".
6. Click "Reactions" to off, then on.
   *You will see* "Ilvara's reactions are off", then "on".
   Covered by `the_switch_turns_the_member_s_reactions_off_and_on`.
7. Through MCP, set a reaction the form cannot write (nested criteria), with Ilvara's `id`:
   `{"Party":{"Tactics":{"PutReaction":{"member":<id>,"entry":null,"set":{"name":"deep","action":{"Spell":"base:spell:shield"},"trigger":"Attacked","when":{"All":[{"Any":[{"Is":{"Hp":{"who":"Me","cmp":"Lt","percent":10}}},{"Is":{"Hp":{"who":"Me","cmp":"Lt","percent":20}}}]},{"Is":{"Hp":{"who":"Me","cmp":"Lt","percent":30}}}]}}}}}}`.
   Click Edit on the new row.
   *You will see* "Set elsewhere: it can be removed here, not edited", every form control dim with it;
   Remove and New stay live. Click Remove on it.
   Covered by `a_reaction_set_deeper_loads_locked_and_may_only_be_removed`.
8. Press Esc.
   *You will see* the sheet again; Esc once more, the street.
   Covered by `tests/sheet.rs::the_column_opens_the_sheet_beside_it_and_esc_steps_back_from_tactics`.
