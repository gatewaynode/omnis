# Acceptance: the full log across loads (M8.5 7f)

This is the script for accepting M8.5 sub-step 7f (`tasks/plans/m8.5-save-model.md`). The full log (L) now
outlives a load. The engine's log is the **party's log**: the newest 16,384 events, each stamped with the party's
clock and map. The character save carries the events a full log tells, and after a load the window **retells**
the full log from them, in today's words.

**Owner's choices (2026-10-18):**
- **Size:** the log keeps 16,384 events.
- **What's saved:** the save leaves out the three things each step makes that the full log never shows:
  - `Visible`, what came into view;
  - `EncounterCheck`, the roll for a random encounter;
  - `TimeAdvanced`, the clock moving on.

  They stay in memory, so `events.tail` is unchanged.
- **Save size:** a character save may be 8 MiB (`MAX_SAVE_BYTES`).

**Measured** (the golden fight's events, the golden walk's interactions):
- **The log:** about 138 B per event, so a full log is 2.27 MB. With every event kept it would have been about
  13 MB, because `Visible` alone is about 2.8 KB per step.
- **A full journal:** 5,000 interactions × 347 B, about 1.74 MB.
- **The worst-case save:** 4.05 MB, about half of the 8 MiB.

**What is not retold:**
- the window's own lines ("Saved …", "Loaded …");
- a search's "Within reach …" and "On the ground …" summaries, which describe the view at that moment;
- the names of members who left the party before the save.

A fold leaves the log alone.

**Start.** Run `just run --windowed --pack packs/base --pack packs/test`, start a new game, and make a party.

## Steps
1. Walk out of the town until a fight starts, and fight it out. Rest (camp), then go back to town and buy
   something. **Search** once somewhere. Press **L**.
   *You will see* every line you saw along the way, each stamped "Day N HH:MM".
2. Esc, **Save**, then quit the game. Launch it again the same way and **Load**.
   *You will see* the full log (L) with the same lines and stamps as step 1, and below them "Loaded
   .omnis/quick.ron: …". The search's own lines ("Searched …", what was found) are there; its "Within reach"
   line is not.
3. In the full log, type a word into **Filter**, say a monster's name or "Day 1".
   *You will see* only the lines holding every word, as before. Typing should not lag.
4. Quit, launch with the objects pack as well (`--pack crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/objects`), start a new game
   with devtools on, and make a party. Use the debug page to teleport to `objects:map:yard` (0, 1) facing North,
   Search until the lever shows, and Use it. Press **Fold** on the debug World page, then **Save** and **Load**.
   *You will see* the lines from before the fold still in the full log, then "The world has moved on" and the
   load's line.

## What the tests cover
- **`omnis-sim/tests/history.rs` (7):**
  - each event is stamped with the clock and map it was said at, across the golden walk;
  - the log keeps the newest 16,384, and `events.tail` answers with events, capped there;
  - a save carries the told events with their stamps, and a load gives them back;
  - a save holding more than the cap loads its newest;
  - a save without a log loads with none;
  - a full log and full journal fit, with two fifths of the room spare;
  - a save over 8 MiB is refused, not written.
- **Elsewhere in `omnis-sim`:**
  - `disk.rs`: both goldens save and load after every command with their logs; a v7 conversion has no log;
  - `fold.rs`: the folded world loads with its log.
- **`omnis-vector/tests/full_log.rs`:**
  - retelling the party's log gives exactly the lines the session said live, over a walk and a fight;
  - a save and load into a session with another game's history gives back every line at its clock.
- **`omnis-vector/tests/game_io.rs`:** a save larger than a pack file loads, and one over 8 MiB is refused for
  its size.
