# Acceptance: a stair's direction (M8.5 step 1c)

This is the script for accepting M8.5 step 1c (`tasks/plans/m8.5-stairs.md`), built to your answers of
2026-10-12:
- **Where it is written:** each map lists its stairs, one entry per tile: `stairs: [(level, x, y, rise)]`.
- **What it does:** a stair up is taken only by stepping onto it from its foot, moving the way it rises. A stair
  down is taken only from its head, moving against the rise. From any other side, stepping onto it is an ordinary
  step onto the tile.
- **It is required:** a stair with no entry, an entry that isn't on a stair, an entry written twice, a foot or
  head that can't be stepped from, and a pair whose rises differ are all refused at load.

**Changed in the test tower** (`crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/levels`):
- **The tower's stair moved.** It is now at (2, 0) and rises west. Its foot is the grass at (3, 0), through a new
  opening in the tower's east wall. Its stair down on the upper floor is entered from the tower floor at (1, 0).
  On the old spot, (1, 0), the stair could not be reached from its foot.
- **The courtyard stair rises north.** Its foot is (4, 4), and its head is the bridge at (4, 2).

## In the window
1. Run `just run --windowed --pack packs/base --pack packs/test --pack crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/levels`.
   Start a new game. In the debug panel's World page, pick `levels:map:tower`, set x 4, y 4, Level
   "Test Tower (0)", facing North, press Go, and close the panel.
   *You will see* the courtyard stair in front of you as a solid flight rising away from you, its treads facing
   you. The minimap marks it with a chevron pointing north.
2. Step forward onto the stair.
   *You will see* the view rise onto the bridge ("Moved to (4, 3) on Upper floor (1) facing North").
3. Step north onto the bridge, turn around, and step south onto the stair.
   *You will see* yourself back in the courtyard: the stair down is taken from its head.
4. Walk to (3, 3), face east, and step onto the courtyard stair from the side.
   *You will see* an ordinary step: you stand on the stair's tile in the courtyard. Step forward again, or step
   back and come at it from (4, 4).
5. Walk to (4, 0), face west, and look at the tower.
   *You will see* the stair just inside the tower's new east opening, rising west, solid. The minimap's chevron
   points west.
6. Step west twice: onto (3, 0), then onto the stair.
   *You will see* the view rise to the upper floor at (2, 0). Step west onto (1, 0), turn around, and step east
   onto the stair: you go back down.
7. On the ground, walk in through the tower's door to (2, 1), face north, and step onto the stair from the side.
   *You will see* an ordinary step. Backing onto it from (3, 0), while facing east, takes you up: the direction
   of the step counts, not the way you face.
8. Quit, and run `just run --windowed` with no packs named.
   *You will see* the shipped maps play as before; they have no stairs.
