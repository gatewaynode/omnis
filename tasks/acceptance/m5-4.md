# Acceptance: the editor's entry and plan view (M5.4)

The fourth step of M5 Editor v1 (`tasks/plans/m5-editor-v1.md` §Client side): the editor opens in the game, with
no game running, and shows a map from above. Nothing is painted yet (M5.5), no preview (M5.7), no Playtest (M5.8).

## What was built
- **Entry.** A fourth Title button, **Editor**, and `--editor [map-id]`. The editor runs with no `Session`: no HUD,
  no pad, no minimap, no 3D view; the pointer is never grabbed over it. Esc, or the tools' **Menu** button, opens
  the editor's menu (Resume, Settings, Back to title, Quit); Back to title closes the editor.
- **The layer** (`shell/editor.rs`), three columns: the tools (the map choice and **Open**, the level choice, Zoom
  -/+, Centre, Face left/right, the zoom in pixels a cell, Menu), the plan (one image of the cells in view, the
  cursor a bordered square over it), the inspector (the map's id and size, the level, the cursor's cell with its
  facing, terrain, height, four edges and any cliff, the start, placements on the cell, and the loader's verdict).
- **The plan** (`editor/plan.rs`, Bevy-free): terrain colours shaded by height, no ground dark, walls, doors,
  ramps, cliffs (from the last valid resolve), stair, ladder and hole marks, the start as a white diamond. Zooms
  4 to 32 pixels a cell; a map just opened is fitted to the pane.
- **Input.** A left click on the plan puts the cursor on the cell; the arrow keys move it; `[` and `]` zoom; C
  centres; Q and E turn the facing. The keys are the editor's only while no screen stands over it.
- **The socket.** `editor.*` ops act on the open editor (the one you see) and the layer follows; while the editor
  is closed they answer `Failed` "the editor is not open".

## Steps (in the window)
1. Run `just run --windowed --pack packs/base --pack packs/test --pack crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/levels`.
   *You will see* the title with four buttons: New game, Load quick save, Settings, **Editor**, then Quit.
2. Click **Editor**.
   *You will see* three columns: "EDITOR  <the levels pack's root>" with the map choice at its first entry,
   a black plan, and "INSPECTOR  No map is open." No HUD, no pad, no minimap. Move the mouse: the pointer stays.
3. Click **Open**.
   *You will see* the tower from above, fitted to the middle column: grass, the road, the tower's walls, the two
   stair marks (chevrons), the ladder, the white diamond at (0, 2). The cursor square sits on the start. The
   inspector reads "levels:map:tower  7 x 5", "Level Test Tower (0)" (or the level's name), "Cell (0, 2) facing
   east", the terrain, height and edges, "Start, facing east", "The loader takes this map."
4. Click cells on the plan.
   *You will see* the cursor square jump to each, and the inspector change with it: the stair at (2, 0) names
   "stair up"; a wall side reads "wall"; the road reads "road".
5. Press the arrow keys, then `]` twice, `[` three times, and C.
   *You will see* the cursor step a cell at a time and stop at the map's edge; the plan grow and shrink (the tools
   say the pixels a cell); Centre put the cursor's cell in the middle of the pane.
6. Step the **Level** choice with its arrows.
   *You will see* the cellar (-1), the upper floor (1) and the roof (2) in turn, their no-ground cells dark, the
   bridge and the hole on the upper floor; the inspector's "Level" line follows. The cursor stays where it is.
7. Press Q and E.
   *You will see* the inspector's "facing" turn. Nothing else changes yet (the preview is M5.7).
8. Press Esc.
   *You will see* the editor's menu: Resume, Settings, Back to title, Quit. Settings opens over it and comes
   back. Press Esc again to resume. Click the tools' **Menu** button: the same menu.
9. Step the **Map** choice (the arrows) to another entry and click Open.
   *You will see* that map; the levels pack has one, the test pack's are reached by running with
   `--pack packs/base --pack packs/test` (the last pack named is the one edited).
10. Quit and run `just run --windowed --editor test:map:dungeon`.
    *You will see* the dungeon open at once, the cursor on its start (1, 0).

## Steps (over MCP, the game running at step 3 or 10)
11. `editor_status` *You will see* `editor` with the open map's id.
12. `editor_open` with `map: "test:map:meadow"` (with the test pack loaded).
    *You will see* the window change to the meadow as the reply comes: the socket acts on the editor you see.
13. Back at the title (Esc, Back to title), `editor_status`.
    *You will see* `Failed`: "the editor is not open; open it from the title or with --editor".
