# Acceptance: solid occlusion (M8.5 step 3)

This is the script for accepting M8.5 step 3 (`tasks/plans/m8.5-occlusion.md`), built to your answers of
2026-10-12:
- **Fills first, then measure.** Dark solid fills now sit under the existing lines. Line meshes were to be built only
  if the measurement said the lines were too slow, and it did not.
- **The levels above and below are solid.** They are hidden where your own level's floors and ceilings cover them,
  and show through open air, holes, stairwells and shafts.
- **The automap still records what the forward cone sees.** Nothing in the engine, the saves or the protocol
  changed, and the replays did not move.

It also repeats 1b's stair, ladder and hole walk (`tasks/acceptance/m8.5-1b.md`), which you could not test without
the fills.

**Departures from the plan:**
- **Stairs stay lines, like ladders.** A stair's flight always climbs north to south, because the map has no
  direction for it. A solid slab under it hid the view of anyone who came at it from the south, the slab's high end
  (the courtyard stair). Giving stairs a direction is a question for you, below.
- **Two 1b drawing bugs fixed, each test-first.**
  - A wall beside a tile with no ground reached down to height 0. The bridge's railings stood on the road.
  - A stair down was drawn falling north to south, so it crossed its stair up as an X. Both now draw one flight.

## Part 1: the fills
1. Run `just run --windowed`. Start a new game and walk the town.
   *You will see* the walls as dark faces under their green lines: nothing behind a wall shows through it. Door
   frames and their crosses are drawn on the door's face, and an open door shows the street through its opening.
   The floor grid is drawn on the ground, not hidden under it.
2. Walk into the dungeon (through the town's portal, as before).
   *You will see* the corridors closed in: side passages show only where a wall is missing. The rock blocks are
   solid boxes.
3. Quit, and run `just run --windowed --pack packs/base --pack packs/test --pack crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/heights`.
   Start a new game, open the debug panel (Esc, then Debug, the "World" page), pick `heights:map:hills`, set x 7,
   y 4, facing West, press Go, and close the panel.
   *You will see* the hill at (2, 4) and the terrace's cliff as solid rock faces with their hatching. Nothing behind
   the hill shows through it. Walk up the ramp onto the terrace and look down (mouse look or PageDown): the low
   ground shows below the cliff's edge.
4. With the window at 5120×1440 (borderless, without `--windowed`), and `--frame-stats` added to the command,
   stand on the hills and read the fps and ms from the HUD.
   *Write down* the numbers. Step 4 measured 145 fps (6.9 ms) there. The floor is not set yet (X20); this is a
   reading.

## Part 2: the tower (1b's walk, repeated)
5. Run `just run --windowed --pack packs/base --pack packs/test --pack crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/levels`.
   Start a new game. In the debug panel's World page, pick `levels:map:tower`, set x 6, y 2, Level "Test Tower (0)",
   facing West, press Go, and close the panel.
   *You will see* the road running west, with the bridge above it as a dark slab with green railings standing on
   it. The hole beside the bridge is outlined in red, and you see the sky through it. The tower is a dark block with
   its yellow ladder inside. Nothing of the upper floor shows through the bridge.
6. Walk west to (4, 2), under the bridge, and look up.
   *You will see* the bridge's dark underside overhead, and the hole's opening beside it.
7. Walk to (4, 4), turn north, and step onto the courtyard stair at (4, 3).
   *You will see* the stair's treads, then the view rise onto the bridge. The event lines read "Moved to (4, 3)
   facing North" and "Moved to (4, 3) on Upper floor (1) facing North". The road below is hidden by the bridge's
   floor, except through the hole.
8. Step off the stair north onto the bridge, then turn and step back onto it.
   *You will see* nothing happen on the step off; stepping back onto the stair takes you down to the courtyard.
9. Go back up, walk north to (4, 2), turn east toward the hole at (5, 2), and look down.
   *You will see* the road through the hole's opening, and nothing past its rim. Step onto the hole: the view drops
   to the road ("Moved to (5, 2) on Test Tower (0) facing East").
10. Walk into the tower through its door (from the road at (2, 2), north to (2, 1)) and look west at the ladder at
    (1, 1). Look up and down.
    *You will see* the ladder rising through an opening in the floor above, and going down through an opening at
    your feet. The rest of the floor and the floor above are solid.
11. Step onto the ladder and use Space, Climb down, then Climb up three times.
    *You will see* the cellar (its floor solid, the shaft open above you), the ground, the upper floor, then the
    roof. On each level the other levels are hidden except up and down the shaft.
12. Climb down to the upper floor, step east to (2, 1) and north to (2, 0), turn west, and step onto the stair down
    at (1, 0).
    *You will see* the stairwell open below you before you step. Afterwards you are on the ground at (1, 0).
13. Quit, and run `just run --windowed` with no packs named.
    *You will see* the town, the meadow and the dungeon play as before.

## Covered by tests
- `tests/fill.rs` (15 tests):
  - every cell of every shipped map filled, and no fill on a tile with no ground;
  - walls from their feet to their top; a closed door solid, an open one open under its lintel; blocks; cliff faces
    between their rims;
  - the hole's opening and rim; the stairwell and the ladder shaft open through each floor; no ceiling over a way up
    (on a copy of the tower with every terrain roofed);
  - sight lines through the fills:
    - from the road, the bridge's underside shows and what lies over the bridge does not;
    - from the bridge's edge, the road shows through the hole, and the road under the bridge does not;
    - from the ground, the cellar shows only down the shaft;
    - the hills hide some ground from the start;
    - no floor line is buried under the floor it lies on;
  - the scene holds the neighbours' fills; the mesh holds every triangle.
- `tests/scene.rs`: a wall beside no ground stands on its floor; a stair pair draws one flight.
- The capture's timing mode: `shell::capture` and `shell::session` unit tests.
