# Acceptance: party creation (parity P3)

The script for your acceptance of parity step P3 (`tasks/plans/p3.md`). A new game from the title
now makes its party before play. What a member may be comes from the engine's new creation view
(`creation.get`), so the screen and an agent read the same rules.

## Part 1: to party creation
1. `just run --windowed`, then New game, type `dragon` as the seed, Start.
   *You will see* "CREATE YOUR PARTY   0 of 6 members". The left column has Name (underlined),
   Race ‹ Dwarf (hill dwarf) ›, Class ‹ Cleric ›, Background ‹ Acolyte ›, Alignment ‹ Lawful Good ›,
   then "Skills (pick 2)": History, Medicine and Persuasion, with Insight and Religion dim and
   reading "from the background". The right column has STR to CHA, each with ‹, a slider, 8 and ›,
   and "cost 0"; CON shows "+2" and WIS "+1". Below them: "0 points spent of 27", "Party",
   "No one yet". Add member reads "Give the character a name", Begin reads "Add at least one
   member" (both dim), and Back is live. The HUD and the corner buttons are hidden.
   Covered by `tests/screens.rs::a_party_is_made_through_the_screen_then_play_begins` and
   `tests/create_party.rs::the_screen_offers_what_the_creation_view_allows`.

## Part 2: one member
1. Type `Brenna`. Step Class to Fighter. Drag STR's slider to the right end, then click DEX's › six
   times.
   *You will see* STR at 15 with "cost 9" and DEX at 14 with "cost 7"; "16 points spent of 27".
   Add member now reads "Pick 2 skills".
2. Click Athletics and Perception.
   *You will see* "[x] Athletics", "[x] Perception", and the other skills dim with "2 picked". Add
   member is live, and the form fits in the window (the scores' "cost N" on their own rows).
3. Click Add member.
   *You will see* "1 of 6 members", "Brenna" under Party, and the form blank again (Dwarf, Cleric,
   all 8s). Begin is live. The name field shows a green cursor; type the next name straight away
   (owner's test, 2026-10-11: this failed once, see `tasks/TODO.md` P3).

## Part 3: what the screen refuses
1. Step Class to Rogue (Background stays Acolyte).
   *You will see* "Skills (pick 4)" and Insight dim, "from the background". The other client let
   you pick it, and the engine then refused the member with "skills: Insight is already
   proficient".
2. Name the rogue, set STR, DEX and CON to 15, then INT to 9.
   *You will see* Add member read "28 points spent of 27". Set INT back to 8, pick four skills, and
   add the rogue.
   Covered by `add_says_why_not_in_the_engine_s_order` and
   `picks_follow_the_class_and_what_the_race_and_background_grant`.

## Part 4: begin, fall, start again
1. Click Begin.
   *You will see* the town, the HUD and the corner buttons; Esc now opens the pause.
2. Fall (through MCP: `sim_command` with `{"Dev": {"SetHp": ...}}` to 0 for both members), then
   Start again.
   *You will see* the same two members, Brenna and the rogue, at the town's start.
   Covered by `start_again_makes_the_same_party_and_after_a_load_the_save_s_game_anew`.
3. Optional: Save in the pause, start another game from the title, Load from its pause, fall, and
   Start again.
   *You will see* party creation for the save's own game (its seed), not the game you loaded over.
   Before P3 this restarted the wrong seed.

## Part 5: leaving and agents
1. From the title, New game, Start, then press Esc on party creation.
   *You will see* the new-game form again, with your seed still typed.
   Covered by `esc_on_party_creation_returns_to_the_filled_form`.
2. Through MCP, call `creation_get`.
   *You will see* the slots (6), `name_max` 32, `score_min` 8, `score_max` 15, `points` 27, the
   costs `[0,1,2,3,4,5,7,9]`, and every race (bonuses, grants), class (`choose`, `skill_list`) and
   background (grants).
   Covered by `omnis-sim/tests/creation_view.rs` and `omnis-mcp/tests/replies.rs`.
