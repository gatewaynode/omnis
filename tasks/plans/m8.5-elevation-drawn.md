## The ground (`crates/omnis-vector/src/ground.rs`, new, Bevy-free)
- **`Ground::from_map(&MapData) -> Ground`:** one entry per cell, holding its height `h / 8` (a wall is 1.0) and
  four corner heights (NW, NE, SE, SW).
- **Corners shared where joined.** Up to four cells meet at a grid corner.
  - **Joined:** two cells side by side are joined across their edge when it is neither a wall nor a cliff in either
    direction. A door, an open edge and a ramp all join.
  - **Splits:** a one-way edge is split too, so it shows as a ledge.
  - **The corner value:** the cells joined around a corner form groups (a union of at most four). Each cell's value
    for that corner is the mean height of its group. Joined cells therefore share the corner exactly, and split cells
    keep their own.
- **The cell's centre is at its own height.** A cell's surface is four triangles from the centre (at `h`) to each
  edge's two corners. So the camera over a centre is at exactly the rule's height, and a slope between cells runs
  through the shared corners.
  - A ramp of 16 reads as a smooth climb across its two cells.
  - A walkable step of 2 reads as a gentle slope.
- **`Ground::height(x, z) -> f32`** samples that surface (the cell under the point, then its triangle). Outside the
  map it gives the nearest cell's height.
- **On a flat map** every corner and centre is 0.0.

## Lines on the ground (`geometry.rs`)
`extract` gains the ground and puts every line on it. The y value comes from `Ground`, never from `0.0`.
- **The floor grid:** each cell's north and west sides run between that cell's two corners. A split edge also gets the
  neighbour's side at the neighbour's corners, so both rims show.
- **Cliffs, `SegKind::Cliff` (new):** on each split edge that is a cliff (not a wall), a rock face.
  - Drawn: the two rims, verticals at both ends, and hatching of vertical lines every quarter cell between the rims
    (the "hatching on rock" of presentation-PRD §5).
  - Colour: a provisional rock tone in `render.rs` `colour` (bright enough to read, distinct from walls). The colour
    roles come in step 5.
  - Always drawn: the grid setting does not hide it.
- **Ramps, `SegKind::Ramp` (new):** tread lines parallel to the ramp edge at quarter cells across both half cells,
  lying on the ground. Always drawn.
- **Walls and doors:**
  - Each end's foot is the lower of the two sides' corner heights there.
  - The top is flat at the higher cell's height + `wall_height` (1.0). This is the engine's sight rule, top = higher
    floor + 8, so what hides you is what you see.
  - Doors keep their frame proportions measured from the foot.
- **Blocks (pillars, trees):** a box from the cell's height to height + 1.0.
- **Barriers (water):** the cross between the cell's corners.
- **Ceilings:** flat at the cell's height + 1.0.
- **Signatures:** the helpers `edge` and `boxed` take a base height. Their unit tests are updated with it.

## The camera follows the ground (`shell/render.rs`)
- **What is drawn with:** `Lines` keeps the `Ground` beside the segments, rebuilt with them. `rebuild` already runs
  on a map change.
- **The eye:** `follow` sets the camera's y to the eye, which comes from a pure `ground::eye(ground, pose)`:
  `height(x, z) + EYE_HEIGHT`.
- **Smoothing:** the y is eased toward the eye with a time constant of about 0.1 s, at once under reduced motion. A
  portal or teleport onto other ground moves more than a wall height, so it snaps.
- **Out of scope:** eye height and field of view stay as they are (presentation-PRD §12.8 is revisited after the
  owner has walked it).

## The minimap shows cliffs and ramps (`minimap.rs`)
- **Where the edges come from:** the minimap draws from the automap. For each known tile, the cliff and ramp edges
  come from that tile's `MapData` cell, as `geometry` already reads cells for the 3D view.
  - An edge is drawn once (by `EdgeKey`) when either side is known and a cliff faces either way.
  - Walls and doors still win the edge.
- **How they look:** a cliff is drawn along the full edge in a `CLIFF` rock colour. A ramp gets a short tick across
  the edge's middle in a `RAMP` colour. The colours are provisional until step 5.
- **The engine is unchanged:** the automap's `Known` does not grow.

## As built (2026-10-12)

  joins), so walls on a flat map add no lines and flat equality holds.

  draws its own vertical there.

  is unchanged.

  frame and a teleport.

  build). The window's numbers are taken in acceptance.
