- **`labels.rs`**, a port of `service_panel.rs`'s words:
  - `offer_label(data, views, offer)` gives the row label:
    - "A night's rest"
    - "A rumor"
    - "Food for N day"
    - the item's name
    - "{item} ×{count}", with the count from `views.party.inventory`
    - "{name}, {hp} of {max} HP"
    - "{name}: conditions" or "{name}, no conditions"
    - "{name}, alive" or "{name}, dead" (from the `NotDead` refusal)
    - "{name}, level L to L+1"
    - "{name}: {spell}"
  - `offer_caption(command)` gives Take a room, Listen, Buy, Sell, Heal, Cure, Raise, Train, Choose, Learn.
  - `offer_note(offer)` gives "free", the price via `labels::coins`, or "pays X".
  - `service_reason(rejection)` gives "not enough money", "nothing to treat", "not dead", "rested too recently (Hh MMm)", or else the engine's text.
- **Service event lines** (`shell/text.rs::describe`), a port of `service_text.rs`:
  - entering and leaving
  - the room
  - food and the rumour in quotes
  - treated and raised
  - bought and sold
  - deposited and withdrew
  - the level-up line
  - a spell chosen or learned
  
  These feed the HUD lines and the panel's message.

## P4b. The service screen (`screen/service.rs`, replacing the stopgap)
- **The title** is the service's name. The first row is the money line "Gold X · Bank Y · Food N" (whole gold via `labels::coins`).
- **Panels per kind, side by side**, as in the old client. Each list has a heading span, then one row per offer:
  - The spans are the label.
  - The control is a button `ControlId::Offer(u16)`, an index into `view.offers`, showing the caption. Its `why_not` is `service_reason(refusal)`.
  - The `after` is the note: the price, "free" or "pays X". A refused purchase keeps its price, so you see "Buy: not enough money" next to "10 gold".
  - A list with no offers shows a dim "Nothing to offer". This happens when the smith has nothing to buy and the stores are empty, or no member has a spell pick.
- **The bank's row:**
  - A text field `ControlId::Amount` labelled "Gold pieces", at most 9 digits. Non-digit input is refused, and the field shows the form's value again. The `TooLong` rewrite path is generalised to any refused text.
  - `ControlId::Deposit` and `ControlId::Withdraw` send `Deposit`/`Withdraw { amount: gold * 100 }`.
  - **Their dim reasons:**
    - With no amount typed: "Type an amount".
    - Otherwise, a trial of the command mapped through `service_reason`. For example `BankShort` gives "The bank holds 40 gold" and `CannotAfford` gives "not enough money".
  - The amount lives in `Forms.bank`. It is cleared when a service is entered, through the `Forms::accepted` hook watching `Service` and the service id.
- **Using a row:** `apply(Offer(i))` returns `Effect::Command { command: offers[i].command.clone(), close: false }`. The view is re-read at apply, so a stale index or a refused offer is refused, with its reason.
- **Leave** is the last row, a button: `Command::Service(Leave)`, with no confirmation. **Esc in a service leaves** (old client parity) through `nav::escape`'s Service case; the pause stays on the Menu button.
- **The outcome message:** `carry_out` sets `forms.message` after an `Effect::Command` from a screen.
  - When accepted, it is the newest line the order added (body role), for example "Bought 1 Dagger for 2 gold".
  - When refused, it is the engine's refusal text (alert role).
  - It is cleared when the screen changes, as now.
- **Long lists scroll.** The screen root gets a `max_height` (the window less the margins), and each panel column scrolls (`Overflow::scroll_y`). A small system turns `MouseWheel` over a panel into its `ScrollPosition`. This is generic, so every screen gains it; the smith's lists are the first that need it.
- **New ids:** `ControlId::{Offer(u16), Amount, Deposit, Withdraw}`.
- **Unchanged:** the party column stays hidden under a screen, so it doesn't show in a service. The column's selection is not used by services; each member/spell pairing is its own row, as in the old client.

## As built (2026-10-11)
P4a `0e02b5c`, P4b `e813c59`, P4c with the docs. Departures, each from the captures or a fact found while
building:
- The bank's field takes 8 digits, not 9: `Deposit`/`Withdraw` are `u32` copper, so at most 42,949,672
  gold (`AMOUNT_MAX_GP`); a larger number is refused (`Refused::OutOfRange`) and the field shows the last.
- Leave sits beside the money line in the first row, not last: a long list scrolls.
- Screen words are ASCII: the interface font has no "·" or "×" ("Gold X  Bank Y  Food N", "Dagger x2").
- `service_reason` also shortens `NotReady` ("0 of 300 XP"), `SpellTooHigh` and `BankShort`.
- The outcome is cleared by `Forms::shown` when the top screen changes, which also clears the bank's
  amount on entering a service (not through `Forms::accepted`).
- The screen's height is capped at 64% of the window while a game is played (95% otherwise).
- The tests went into `tests/service.rs`, model and shell both; the stopgap test left `tests/shell.rs`.
