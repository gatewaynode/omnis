# Plan: M8.5 step 1b, the Z axis (levels in one map, and how they join)

## Context
Step 1a gave each cell a height, and steps 4 and 4b drew it and let the camera look up and down. A map is still one
sheet: the only way to another level is a portal to another map (`test:map:dungeon` → `test:map:depths`). Step 1 of
M8.5 asks for "levels underground and above the ground, and how they join (stairs, ramps, shafts)". Everything after
builds on it: objects with a level (step 2), occlusion (step 3), and M9's generated maps.

**The owner's answers (2026-10-12):**
- **Layers in one map:** one map file holds several levels, and a position gains a level number. This was chosen
  over stacking separate maps.
- **Joins:**
  - stairs (two-way, paired automatically);
  - ladders and shafts (climbed with an action);
  - holes (a one-way drop; falling damage stays later, X11).
- **Overlaps drawn:** the levels just above and below are drawn at their real heights, dimmer. The rules stay per
  level: you cannot see, walk or fight across levels.
- **Test data:** a new fixture pack. The shipped maps stay as they are.

## The model
- **`Level(i8)`**, a newtype in `omnis-core` (`id.rs`/`geom.rs`).
  - 0 is the map's own layout. Negative levels are below ground, positive above.
  - It is called a level, not a layer, because the automap already has `layer` bits in the protocol. It is a
    newtype so it is never confused with a character's level.
- **`Position { map, level, x, y, facing }`:**
  - `level` is `#[serde(default, skip_serializing_if = "Level::is_ground")]`, so a level-0 position serialises
    exactly as before.
  - `Place` (the string-id form in events) gains the same field, written the same way.
  - Every constructor is updated: the compiler lists them, so no call site keeps level 0 silently.

## Data (`omnis-data`)
**The format.** `MapDef.levels: Vec<LevelDef>` (`#[serde(default)]`) has these fields:
- `level: i8`: unique, not 0, since level 0 is the map's own `layout` and `heights`;
- `name: String`, for the HUD ("Cellar", "Roof");
- `base: i16`: the level's height in eighths, added to every cell's height, so a cellar is `base: -8` with flat
  heights;
- `layout: Vec<String>`: the same grammar and size as the map's;
- `heights: Vec<String>`: optional, relative to `base`.

**No ground.** A reserved tile glyph `_` means there is no cell on this level there: air above ground, solid rock
below.
- It is refused as a terrain glyph. (`.` was planned, but the test dungeon's floor uses it.)
- A step into it is refused, with a new `BlockReason::NoGround` ("Blocked: no ground").
- It does not block sight. It is not drawn.

**Joins.** `Terrain.join: Option<Join>` (`#[serde(default)]`). A join belongs to the terrain, so the author gives
stair cells their own glyph.
- **`Join::Up` / `Join::Down`** (stairs):
  - Stepping onto the cell takes the party to the same (x, y) on the next level up or down, keeping its facing.
  - "Next" means the nearest level number above or below that the map has.
  - Arriving by a join never fires the cell you land on, so the stair at the far end doesn't send you straight back.
    You step off and on again to return.
- **`Join::Ladder`** (a ladder or a shaft):
  - Standing on it, a new command `Command::Climb(Vertical::Up | Vertical::Down)` moves the party one level, to the
    same (x, y).
  - It is refused (`Refused: no ladder that way`) unless the cell there is also a ladder, or is passable at the top
    or bottom end.
  - It costs the destination terrain's `step_minutes`.
- **`Join::Hole`:** stepping onto it drops the party to the same (x, y) on the next level down. It is one way.

**Checks at load, each with its message:**
- a stair, ladder or hole with no level beyond;
- a stair, ladder or hole whose landing cell is no ground or impassable;
- a level number repeated or 0;
- a level layout of the wrong size;
- a `base` plus height out of `±MAX_HEIGHT`;
- more than `MAX_LEVELS` (16) levels;
- encounters, sites and portals placed on a level that doesn't exist.

**Placing things on a level.** Encounters, sites and portals gain `level` (`#[serde(default)]`). A portal can land
on a level (`to_level`).

**Cells per level.** `MapData` holds the cells for every level. `cell(level, x, y)` replaces `cell(x, y)`. The old
signature is removed, not kept beside it, so every reader states its level. `MapData::levels()` lists them in order.

**Where the code goes.** A new module `omnis-data/src/levels.rs` parses and checks levels and joins; `map.rs` is
already 714 lines. Each refusal is added to the bad-pack corpus.

## The engine (`omnis-sim`)
- **Movement (`apply.rs`):**
  - `landing()` reads the party's level.
  - A step onto `Up`, `Down` or `Hole` emits `Moved` onto the cell, then a second `Moved` to the other level, as a
    portal does. The client already snaps on two `Moved` events (`bind.rs` substep).
  - Climb has its own path. It is valid while exploring and refused in a fight.
  - Stairs and ladders ignore `climb`, as ramps do.
- **Sight (`visibility.rs`):** per level. The cone, `line_of_sight`, `above` and `depth` read the party's level.
  Cells with no ground are transparent and count as no floor.
- **State** (as built: `SAVE_SCHEMA` stays 7. The other levels' state went in new fields written only when they
  hold something, `MapState.level_doors` and `Automap.levels`, so a level-0 save is byte-identical, the replays
  did not move and no migration was needed. The plan as written was:) **`SAVE_SCHEMA` 8:**
  - `Automap.maps` becomes `BTreeMap<MapId, BTreeMap<(Level, u16, u16), Known>>`.
  - `MapState.open_doors` becomes `(Level, u16, u16, Facing)`.
  - The migration `v7_to_v8` puts every old key on level 0, with a `tests/saves/v7.ron` fixture and an
    `a_schema_7_save_migrates` test.
  - After a load, the position check covers the level.
- **Views and the JSON tier (protocol 2, additive):**
  - `ViewportModel.level`, and `level` and `name` for the party's level.
  - `KnownTile.level`.
  - `map.text` and `automap.get` take an optional `level`, defaulting to the party's level.
  - `map.text` shows join glyphs as their terrain glyph and the party's level only.
  - `Command::Climb`, `Join` in the views, and `BlockReason::NoGround`.
  - `DevCommand::Teleport` gains `level` (`#[serde(default)]`). The dev schema number moves, with the proof's numbers.
  - The MCP tool schemas and `docs/api.md` (§5, §7.10) get a changelog entry.
- **Replays:**
  - The automap's key changes the saved world's shape, so the walk and fight replays are rebaselined for that alone.
  - The proof: before rebaselining, the replays reach the recorded worlds once the new world is written back in the
    v7 shape (a test helper that drops the level from the keys). This shows that the behaviour did not move.

## The client (`omnis-vector`)
- **The mirror:** `collide::blocks` reads the party's level and mirrors `NoGround`. The agreement test runs on the
  new fixture, level by level.
- **The ground and lines:**
  - `Ground::from_map(map, level)` and `extract(map, level, ...)` work per level, at absolute heights (`base` +
    height).
  - `Lines` holds the party's level and its neighbours. Neighbour segments are drawn dimmer (a factor on the colour,
    provisional until step 5) and cached per (map, level).
  - The camera's eye uses the party's level. A change of level of more than a wall snaps, as a portal does.
  - Cells with no ground draw nothing: no floor grid, no walls of their own.
  - A level's ceiling grid is drawn only where the cell has a ceiling and no cell of the level above sits on it.
- **Joins drawn**, so the player can see them (LESSONS: "check the player can perceive it"). The new `SegKind`
  values are `Stair`, `Ladder` and `Hole`:
  - a stair: treads rising across the cell to the level above, or falling for down;
  - a ladder: two rails and rungs from the floor to the ceiling;
  - a hole: the cell's outline hatched inward, with the level below visible through it.
- **The minimap:**
  - It shows the party's level only.
  - Join cells get a glyph-like mark: up and down chevrons, a ladder's rungs, a hole's cross.
  - The level's name is shown under the minimap when the map has more than one level.
- **The HUD:** the place line gains the level ("tower, Roof (2), 3,1 facing east").
- **The Actions menu (Space):** "Climb up" and "Climb down", with the reason when it can't be done. This is the mouse
  entry point for ladders; there is no new key.
- **The debug panel's World page:** a Level choice listing the map's levels, used by Go.
