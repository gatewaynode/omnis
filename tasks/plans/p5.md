# Plan: parity P5, inventory, spells on the road, the message line

## Context
P5 is the next parity step after P4 (accepted 2026-10-11). The party column's Inventory and Spells
buttons still read "Not built yet (P5)". The explore message line was deferred to P5 in
presentation-ARCHITECTURE §6.6.

**Owner answers (2026-10-11):**
- **Layout:** the member's kit and the party's stores side by side, with a member switch at the top.
- **Targets:** the party column stays visible beside the inventory and the cast screen. Its selection
  receives a Give, a potion and a targeted spell, as in the old client.
- **After a cast:** the cast screen stays open and the outcome shows on it.
- **Message line:** built in P5.

**What exists (from exploration):**
- **Engine** (`omnis-sim`), all in `api`:
  - `ItemCommand::{Equip{member,item}, Unequip{member,slot}, Give{giver,receiver,item,count},
    Stow{member,item,count}, Take{member,item,count}, Use{member,item,receiver: Option}}`. There is no Drop.
  - `Command::Cast{caster, spell, target: Target}`.
  - Both are accepted only in Explore or Town (`apply.rs:38-48`).
  - `ItemView{item, name, count, slot, usable, consumable, equipped}`. `MemberView.equipment` is the kit and
    `.worn` the slots; `PartyView.inventory` is the stores.
  - `cast_view(&World,&Data) -> Vec<CastView{caster, spell, name, cost, targets_members, refusal}>`.
  - `SimWorld::trial` already gives the engine's exact refusal for any command, on a copy of the world.
- **Old client:**
  - `inventory_menu.rs`: panes per member plus STORES; "wearing …"; the row marks "worn"/"use"; actions
    Equip/Take off, Use, Stow, Take, Give; counts always 1; Give to the band's selection, Take to the
    selection or the first member.
  - `spell_menu.rs` road cast: "N pt"; notes "need N pt", "needs components", "not here"; "Nobody knows a
    spell for the road."; the target is the selection or the caster.
  - `item_text.rs` and `spell_text.rs`: the log lines.
  - Its buttons are lit by its own rules (`applies`), not trials, so it lets dead or down members,
    two-handed conflicts and dead targets through to the engine's refusal. The vector client
    lights every button by a trial instead, so it shows exactly what the engine will accept.
- **An engine defect, found in exploration:**
  - The problem: `CastView.cost` is `Spell::point_cost()` (`points.unwrap_or(level)`, omnis-data). What the cast
    charges, and what `NotEnoughPoints.need` reports, is `omnis_rules::spell_cost`, which evaluates the
    `spell_points.cost` slot. 13 of the 18 base spells have no `points`.
  - Why it hasn't shown: the shipped slot is `spell_level`, so the two agree today.
  - When it breaks: a pack or a `rules.set` that changes the slot makes the view show the wrong price. That
    is a rule read from data (§4.4), so it is fixed in the engine.
- **Vector client:**
  - `roster::RosterEffect::Open{tool, member, read_only}` is ignored in `shell/roster.rs:92`.
  - The column shows only when no screen is up (`shell/roster.rs::shown`).
  - `Screens.selected` is not visible to screens.
  - `Views` has no `casts`.
  - `shell/text.rs` has no item or cast lines; only `rolllog.rs` words casts, for the fight log.
  - The HUD has no alert colour and no single message line.

## P5a. Engine fix, views and words
- **The engine fix (bug, red test first).**
  - `cast_view` takes each row's cost from `omnis_rules::spell_cost`, run on a copy of the rng stream, as
    the refusal already is.
  - Test (`omnis-sim/tests/api_views.rs`): with `spell_points.cost` set to `spell_level * 2` in a copy of
    the data, Bless's row costs 2, and at 1 point its refusal is `NotEnoughPoints{need: 2}`.
  - The field and protocol 2 are unchanged. `docs/api.md` §7.8 says the cost is the slot's.
  - The replays do not move.
- **`Views.casts: Vec<CastView>`**, refreshed in `Views::of` through a new `SimWorld::casts`.
- **The selection moves into `Forms`** (`Forms.selected`), so screens read it. `Screens::reset` clears it, and
  `shell/roster.rs` sets it.
- **Words (`labels.rs`):**
  - `slot(EquipSlot)` gives "Main hand", "Off hand", "Ranged" or "Body".
  - `count_label(name, n)` gives "Rations x5", or just the name when n = 1. It is ASCII, like P4.
  - `item_reason(&Rejection)` and `cast_reason(&Rejection)` shorten the engine's words where they name a
    member or an item by id:
    - "need N pt", "needs components", "not here";
    - "cannot be worn or wielded", "no hand free for a shield";
    - "does nothing when used";
    - "{name} is dead" and "{name} is down" (with the name, not the number);
    - otherwise the engine's text.
- **Event lines (`shell/text.rs::describe`)**, a port of `item_text.rs` and `spell_text.rs`:
  - items:
    - "{m} wears/wields {item}"
    - "{m} takes off {item}"
    - "{m} gives {what} to {n}", "{m} stows {what}", "{m} takes {what}"
    - "{m} uses {what} (on {n})"
  - spells:
    - "{m} casts {spell} (free / N pt)"
    - "{m} regains N hp, now H"
    - "{spell} settles on {m}" / "{spell} lights the party's way"
    - "{spell} fades from {m}"
    - "{m} lets {spell} go"
  - The fight's roll log (`rolllog.rs`) is unchanged.

## P5b. The inventory and cast screens, with the column beside them
- **Navigation:**
  - New overlays `ScreenId::{Inventory, Cast}`. `Nav::allowed` allows them while exploring and in a fight;
    in a fight they are read-only. They are not allowed in a service, where the column is hidden.
  - Both join `Nav::paused`, so the party cannot walk or meet monsters with one open.
  - Esc closes them (`Nav::escape` closes the top overlay first).
  - `shell/roster.rs` carries out `RosterEffect::Open`: it sets `Forms.owner` to the member and opens the screen.
    Pressing another tool, or another member's tool, while one is open switches to it.
- **The column beside:**
  - `shown` is also true when the top screen is Inventory or Cast.
  - The column's paint writes its placed width to a resource (`Beside`). The screen layer pads its right side
    by that width plus margins, so the screen centres in the room left of the column.
  - Clicking a member in the column selects them, as now. That selection is the target, and the column
    shows which member is selected.
- **Inventory (`screen/inventory.rs`)**:
  - **Header:** title "Inventory", then a row with an `Owner` choice ("< Brenna >", whose kit) and Close,
    then "wearing Longsword, Shield, Chain mail" (in `EquipSlot::ALL` order) or "wearing nothing".
  - **Kit panel:** heading "Kit", then one row per `ItemView`:
    - The label is `count_label`. The after-text is the slot and "worn", or "use".
    - The buttons are only those that apply to the row, by the view's own fields:
      - Equip (when `slot` is set), or Take off when worn (`Unequip{slot}`);
      - Use (when `usable`), on the selection or, with none, the user;
      - Stow;
      - Give, to the selection.
    - "Nothing carried" when the kit is empty.
  - **Stores panel:** heading "Stores", then "60 gp 0 sp 0 cp  food 40", then rows with Take (to the shown
    member). "Nothing in the stores" when empty.
  - **Dim reasons:** every button is lit by a trial of its exact command. A refused one is dim with
    `item_reason`. Give has client wording:
    - with no selection: "Select a member in the party column";
    - when the selection is the giver: "Select another member in the party column".
  - **Counts** are 1, as in the old client.
  - **Ids:** `ControlId::Owner` and `ControlId::Item{at: u16, act: ItemAct}`, where
    `ItemAct = Equip | Unequip | Use | Stow | Give | Take` and `at` indexes the kit or the stores. `apply`
    re-reads the views and sends `Effect::Command{close: false}`.
  - **In a fight:** the rows without buttons, and a note: "In a fight: look here, act from the fight's
    actions".
- **Cast on the road (`screen/cast.rs`)**:
  - **Header:** title "Spells", then the `Owner` choice (the caster) and Close.
  - **Rows:** one per `Views.casts` row of the caster. The label is the spell's name. The after-text is
    "N pt", plus "on {target}" for a spell that targets members. The button is Cast.
  - **Target:** the selection, or the caster when nothing is selected. The parity row says the same.
  - **Dim reasons:** the view's refusal through `cast_reason` when it has one, otherwise a trial of the
    exact `Command::Cast`. The trial also covers a dead or down caster and a dead target, which the view
    does not.
  - **Empty texts:** "{name} knows no spell for the road", or "Nobody knows a spell for the road." when no
    one does.
  - **After a cast:** the screen stays open and the outcome shows on it (P4's `Session::ordered`).
  - **In a fight:** read-only, with a note.
- **The cost of trials:**
  - `screen::content` runs up to three times a frame. A full kit can mean about 40 trials a build, each
    cloning the world.
  - Measure it first (100 builds of a full inventory, release profile).
  - Over 1 ms a build, `Screens` keeps the last built screen, keyed on a `Session.revision` counter that
    `refresh` bumps and on the forms' state, and builds again only when either changes.
- **The column's tool buttons:** `roster::why_not` loses "Not built yet (P5)" for Inventory and Spells.
  Spells is dim "No spells for the road" when the member has no `casts` rows.

## P5c. The message line
- **`Session.message: Option<Span>`:**
  - `note` sets it to the newest line from the events, skipping `Moved`. A batch that only moved clears it.
  - A refusal from `ordered`, or from the movement path, sets it in the alert role.
- **Drawing:** `shell/message.rs` draws one text node at the bottom centre, between the movement pad and
  the right-hand buttons, wrapping in that width.
  - It shows only while exploring with no screen up. A screen shows its own outcome, and the fight has its
    own log.
  - It has no timer; the next event, refusal or move replaces it, as in the old client.
- **Unchanged:** the HUD's recent lines.

## Tests (real packs, no mocks)
- **Engine:** the cost test above (red first). Each `CastView` row's cost equals what the cast charges.
- **Model (`tests/inventory.rs`, `tests/cast.rs`, Bevy-free):**
  - The kit and the stores list what the views hold: "wearing …", the slot and "worn"/"use", the empty texts.
  - Every live button sends exactly its command and the engine accepts it. Every dim one carries the
    trial's reason.
  - A two-handed weapon with a shield worn: Equip is dim "no hand free for a shield".
  - A down member's Equip and Use are dim "{name} is down".
  - Give:
    - with no selection, dim "Select a member in the party column";
    - when the selection is the giver, dim "Select another member…";
    - when the selection is another member, live, and the item moves.
  - Take goes to the shown member, and the Owner choice switches the kit.
  - Use with no selection heals the user; with a selection, the selected member.
  - Cast:
    - the rows are the caster's road spells, with "N pt" equal to `CastView.cost`;
    - at 0 points the costed spells are dim "need N pt" and a cantrip stays live;
    - Cure Wounds goes to the selection, or the caster with none;
    - Light is live and lights the way;
    - the empty texts.
  - In a fight: no buttons, the note shown.
  - The event lines for each item and cast event, word for word.
- **App (shell):**
  - The column's Inventory opens the screen with the column still shown beside it, not overlapping
    (`ComputedNode` rects).
  - Selecting a member in the column then pressing Give moves the item, and the panel reports "{m} gives …".
  - Esc closes the screen. The party cannot walk while it is open.
  - Casting keeps the screen open and reports the line.
  - The message line shows the last event, shows a refusal in the alert colour, and clears after a move.
- **Mutations, each to be caught:**
  - Give sent to the giver instead of the selection;
  - a refused action left live (the trial skipped);
  - Take to the selection instead of the shown member;
  - the cast target always the caster;
  - the screen closing after a cast;
  - Inventory not pausing movement;
  - the message line not cleared on a move;
  - the cost from `point_cost` (the engine fix reverted).

## As built (2026-10-11)
- **Commits:** P5a `315b823` (gate 745), P5b + P5c `3795b10` (gate 757/0/13, mutations 8 of 8, Sentrux 8984).
- **No cache.** Measured: about 0.35 ms an inventory build in release, about 3 builds a frame, under the 1 ms
  threshold, so `Screens` builds every frame as before.
- **The selection's reason words** became one `labels::reason(party, &Rejection)` for items and casts, not two.
- **No two-handed melee weapon** is in the packs, so "no hand free for a shield" (`HandsFull`) cannot come up in
  play; its wording is unit-tested only. The acceptance script drops the two-handed step.
- **A potion** is in every fixed member's kit (the acolyte background) and the smith sells one; a potion on a
  member at full HP is accepted and spent, so the script lowers an HP through MCP first.
- **The message line's red refusal** is reachable only by a refused command through `Session::ordered`; every
  button is lit by a trial and MCP's refusals do not reach the line, so in play it is seen in tests only. A
  refused step has no line (the binder counts it).
- **The column's tool row** follows the selection; Inventory and Cast open for the member whose row was
  pressed (`Forms.owner`), and pressing another tool while one is open swaps it (`Nav::show`).
- **At 1280x720** the kit's buttons wrap onto a second line inside each row of the half-width panel.
- **Untouched:** `omnis-app`; replays, `SAVE_SCHEMA` 7, `PROTOCOL` 2.
