## P6a. Engine: known spells with their costs, the fight's cost fix; views and words
- **The fight's cost fix (bug, red test first).**
  - One crate-private helper, `cast_view::cost(world, data, spell) -> u32`: `omnis_rules::spell_cost` on a fresh copy of the `cast` stream, falling back to `point_cost()` on a rule error, as `cast_view` does now. `cast_view` and `spell_views` (`view.rs:311`) both use it.
  - Test (`omnis-sim/tests/api_views.rs`): with `spell_points.cost` set to `spell_level * 2` in a copy of the data, Bless's fight `SpellView.cost` is 2. Red before the change.
- **The known-spell view (§4.4).** `MemberView.known_spells: Vec<KnownSpellView>`, `#[serde(default)]`, additive:
  - `KnownSpellView { spell: String /* id */, name: String /* text key */, level: u8, cost: u32, pay: Cost }`. `cost` is the helper's (what a cast charges); `pay` is the spell's `Cost` (action, bonus action, reaction, free), named `pay` to match `FeatureView.pay`.
  - `party_view` passes `world` down so `member_view` can take the stream copies.
  - Before the names are fixed: grep the wire types for `known_spells:`, `pay:`, `level:`, `cost:`, and run the vocabulary test (seconds) before the gate.
  - Test: every member's `known_spells` row costs what a cast charges with the doubled slot, and its `pay` is the definition's `cost`. A reaction spell (Shield, if a member knows it) reads `Reaction`.
  - Measure `party_view` before and after (it runs on every refresh); report the numbers.
- **`api` re-exports `Row`** (the tactics predicate's front/back) as `omnis_rules::Row`; no wire change.
- **Docs:** `docs/api.md` §7.2 (`known_spells`, `KnownSpellView`), §7.4 (`SpellView.cost` is the slot's), changelog under "Additions within protocol 2". `PROTOCOL` 2, `SAVE_SCHEMA` 7 unchanged; both replays unchanged.
- **Vector views:** `Views.rest: RestView` through a new `SimWorld::rest`, refreshed in `Views::of`.
- **Words (`labels.rs`):**
  - `race`, `background` (by id, like `class`); `ability(Ability)` ("Strength"), `signed(i64)` ("+3", "-1", "+0"); `pay(Cost)` ("action", "bonus action", "reaction", "free").
  - `rest_reason(&Rejection)`: "rested too recently (10h 05m)", "the rest eats N food; the stores hold M", the member refusals with names ("{name} is unhurt", "{name} has no hit dice left", "{name} is dead"), else the engine's text.
  - `tactics_reason(&Rejection)`: "{name} cannot answer that", "no such entry", each `TacticsFault` in a few words ("name too long", "too many conditions", "nested too deep", "percent over 100", "the library is full"), "not loaded: {id}".
- **Event lines (`shell/text.rs::describe`)**, from `service_text.rs` and `spell_text.rs`:
  - "The party rests for the night and eats N food", "The party rests for an hour" ("N hours", "N minutes"), "{name} spends N hit die/dice", "Ambushed after an hour!".
  - "{name}'s reactions are on/off", "{name}'s tactics are set".

## P6b. Camp, and the Camp button
- **Navigation:**
  - New overlays `ScreenId::{Camp, Sheet, Tactics}`, all in `Nav::paused`.
  - One rule for the column: `ScreenId::beside_column()` is true for Inventory, Cast, Camp, Sheet, Tactics. `shell/roster.rs::shown`, `::beside` and `Nav::show` use it in place of their three `matches!`.
  - `allowed`: Camp when the base is none and `views.exploring()` (so never in a service or a fight); Sheet when the base is none (exploring, or a fight, read-only); Tactics when the base is none and there is no fight.
  - `Nav::settle` already drops what is no longer allowed: an ambush during a rest closes the camp and the fight takes over; a fight starting closes Tactics, leaving the Sheet.
  - `Nav::show` replaces an open Inventory, Cast or Sheet with the one asked for. Camp opens with `toggle`. Tactics opens over the Sheet (`Effect::Open`), so Esc and Close return to the Sheet.
- **The Camp button (`shell/controls.rs`):**
  - `Action::Camp` ("Camp  R") in `SIDE` before Actions and Menu; R toggles it too (R is unbound today).
  - A new system lights or dims it every frame by `Nav::may_open(ScreenId::Camp, ..)` (the private `allowed` made public under that name), inserting or removing `Button` and swapping its colours, as `button(.., enabled)` draws a dim one now. No reason text, as the old tool bar (the HUD has no room for one).
- **Camp (`screen/camp.rs`):**
  - **Header:** title "Camp"; a row "The night eats N food; the stores hold M" with Close.
  - **A row per member** (by index into `Views.rest.members` and `party.members`): "{name}  HP h/max  Hit dice l/t dD"; a `Kind::Number` "Spend" (0 to `spendable`, id `ControlId::Dice(slot)`) only when `spendable > 0`; after it a note: "spend N", "unhurt", "no hit dice left" or "cannot spend".
  - **Short rest:** a button with the note "An hour; each member spends the dice chosen". Dim with `rest.refusal`, else "Choose hit dice to spend" when every count is 0, else a trial of `RestCommand::Short` with the non-zero counts.
  - **Long rest:** a button with the note "eight hours: full hit points and spell points, half the hit dice back", dim with `long_refusal` through `rest_reason`, else a trial.
  - **Form:** `Forms.dice: Vec<u8>`, fitted to the members and capped at each `spendable` whenever the screen is built or applied; cleared by `Forms::shown` when the top changes and after a short rest is sent.
  - **After a rest:** the screen stays open with the outcome on it (`Session::ordered`).
- **The column:** Camp stands beside it; its selection is not used by the camp.

## P6c. The character sheet
- **`screen/sheet.rs`**, opened from the column's Sheet for that member (`Forms.owner`), the column beside:
  - **Header row:** title "Sheet"; `Owner` choice "< name >" (the inventory's `owner_choice`), after it "Race Class level" and "member i of n"; Close.
  - **Tab row:** `ControlId::Page` as a `Kind::Choice` of "Stats", "Magic", "Gear" (`Forms.page`, kept between openings); Tactics (`ControlId::Tactics`, `Effect::Open(ScreenId::Tactics)`), dim "Not in a fight" in a fight.
  - **Stats:** "HP h/max  SP s/max  AC n  Proficiency +p  XP x / next" ("max" at the top); "Hit dice l/t dD", then "Ready to train" and "Spell picks N" when they apply; a row per ability "STR 16 +3" (three to a row); "Saves  STR +5  CON +4"; "Skills" then each proficient skill with its bonus, or "none"; "Conditions  a, b" or "none"; "{background}  {alignment}  {age} years".
  - **Magic:** "Casting {Ability}  Points s/max" or "No spellcasting"; "Spells" then a row per `known_spells`: the name, after it "cantrip" or "N pt" and the action cost ("action", "bonus action", "reaction"), or "none"; "Effects" then the member's and the party's effects with "N min" or "next turn", or "none".
  - **Gear:** "Worn and wielded" then a row per slot in `EquipSlot::ALL` order, "Main hand  Longsword" or "-"; "Carried" then "Name xN" per item (`count_label`), or "none".
  - Panels scroll as every screen's do. The sheet has no other actions, so in a fight it only looks.
- **The column:** `roster::why_not` loses "Not built yet (P6)" for Sheet; `Tool::Sheet.screen()` is `ScreenId::Sheet`.

## P6d. Tactics
- **`screen/tactics.rs`** (the screen) and **`screen/tactics_form.rs`** (the draft, a port of `tactics_draft.rs` and the form half of `tactics_panel.rs`, Bevy-free).
  - **Header:** title "Tactics"; `Owner` choice; "Reactions" as a `Kind::Choice` "off"/"on" from `reactions_on`, sending `SetReactions` (trial-lit); Close (back to the Sheet).
  - **The list:** "Declared reactions, in the order they are considered", then "{n}. {action} on {trigger}, when {criteria}" per entry with Edit and Remove (Remove trial-lit), or "None declared".
  - **The form:** "New reaction" or "Editing reaction N"; Action (a choice of `answers` by name, or the note "nothing to declare yet") and Trigger (that answer's triggers); When: "all of these"/"any of these", the note "hold (none: always)", and Add condition (dim at 15 conditions); per condition: Kind (the 8 kinds, words as before), its fields (monster, condition, who "me"/"the subject", comparison "<", "<=", "=", ">=", ">", row "front"/"back"), a `Kind::Number` for kinds that take one (0 to 65535 or 100), and Drop.
  - **Save** sends `PutReaction{entry: editing}` with the name "{action} on {trigger}" cut to 32 bytes; dim with "Nothing this member can declare", "Sixteen reactions at most" (new entry, list full), or the trial's reason through `tactics_reason`. **New** clears the form.
  - **The lock:** an entry the form cannot show (nested criteria, an action or trigger no longer among the answers, an unloaded monster or condition) loads as "Set elsewhere: it can be removed here, not edited"; every form control is dim with that reason, Remove, New, the switch and the member stay live.
  - **Catalogue:** triggers from the answer; monsters and conditions from `data.registry` by label (§6.7: catalogue from `Data`); actions from `TacticsView.answers`.
  - **Form state:** `Forms.tactics: TacticsForm{member, editing, action, trigger, any, conditions, locked}`; reset when the member changes, after a Save or Remove is sent, and by `Forms::shown` when Tactics is opened anew. Every pick is range-checked and every number clamped, as in the old client; `apply` refuses the rest (§6.2).
  - **Ids:** `ControlId::Tactic(TacticAct)` with `TacticAct = Reactions | Edit(u8) | Remove(u8) | Action | Trigger | Join | Add | Kind(u8) | Field(u8, u8) | Number(u8) | Drop(u8) | Save | New`, kept in `tactics_form.rs` as `ItemAct` is in `inventory.rs`.
  - Not allowed in a fight (the fight's React toggle is P7).
