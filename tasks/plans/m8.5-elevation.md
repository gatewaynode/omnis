# Plan: M8.5 step 1a, elevation in the map format (the Z axis is 1b, planned after)

## Context
M8.5 step 1 is the 3D map format. The owner asked to settle how fine elevation is before the Z axis
(2026-10-12). Today every map is flat: `Cell { terrain, walls, doors }`, no height anywhere in
`omnis-data`, `omnis-sim` or the client (the client draws at y = 0, walls 1.0 tall, eye 0.5).
presentation-PRD X10/X11 left the data shape open (§12.1, XR10: "designed with the mechanics work").

**Owner answers (2026-10-12):**
- Height is **per cell, plus ramp edges**.
- **One unit is an eighth of a cell**: a wall's height is 8 units.
- **The climb allowance is per terrain**, and the **terrain entered** decides it, as it already decides the step's minutes.
- **Drops are limited like climbs.** A difference beyond the entered terrain's allowance blocks both ways.
- **A ramp edge crosses any difference up to a cap.** The loader checks the cap: two wall heights, 16 units.
- **Sight: hills hide, and standing high extends how far you see.**

Consequences of these answers, stated so they are seen:
- **An edge can be one-way.** Climbing from scree (allowance 1) to a stone stair (allowance 4) across 3 units is
  allowed, because the stair is entered. Coming back is blocked, because the scree is entered.
- **Sight with height changes what the automap records.** On flat maps the answer must equal today's exactly. A
  test proves this, so the replays' play is unchanged.

## Data (`omnis-data`, data schema stays 1, all fields `#[serde(default)]`)
- **`MapDef.heights: Vec<String>`:**
  - Optional: `height` rows of `width` whitespace-separated signed integers, one per cell.
  - Empty means every cell is at 0, so existing maps are unchanged.
  - Range: `limits::MAX_HEIGHT` (proposed ±4096, which is 512 wall heights) in an `i16`.
  - Errors are named: a row has the wrong count, a number is not a number, or a value is out of range.
- **`Terrain.climb: u16`**, default 2 (a quarter wall): the largest height difference allowed into this terrain.
- **A ramp edge in the layout: `/`.**
  - It works in either position (horizontal or vertical edge), because its position gives its orientation.
  - `/` joins the reserved glyphs. No terrain uses it today.
  - It is invalid on the outer boundary, which must be a wall.
  - A ramp whose two cells differ by more than `MAX_RAMP` (16) is a load error.
- **Parsed `Cell` gains:**
  - `height: i16`;
  - `ramps: Edges` (from the layout);
  - `cliffs: Edges`, derived when the pack loads: side `s` of cell A is a cliff when there is no ramp and
    `|h(B) − h(A)| > climb(terrain(B))`, where B is the neighbour.

  One place computes it (`map.rs` or the loader's resolve); the engine and the client both read it.
- **Limits and the bad-pack corpus:** new rows for a bad height row, a height out of range, a ramp on the boundary,
  a ramp over the cap, and a reserved `/` glyph.
- **The rule slot `sight.reach`** (inputs `depth`, `above`; expression `depth + above / 8`) in the base pack's
  rules.

## Rules (`omnis-sim`)
- **Movement (`apply.rs::landing`):** after the impassable check, a cliff side gives
  `Event::Blocked { reason: Cliff }`.
  - `BlockReason::Cliff` is an additive variant (protocol stays 2, with a changelog line).
  - A portal landing is not affected: portals join any heights.
- **Sight (`visibility.rs`):**
  - `line_of_sight` gains height. The eye is at the party's floor plus 4 units (half a wall).
  - The ray is aimed at the target's floor at its centre and its four corners, as today.
  - Along the existing supercover walk, each crossed edge or cell blocks when its top is above the sight line at
    that point:
    - a wall or closed door: the higher floor beside it plus 8;
    - an opaque terrain: its floor plus 8;
    - the ground: its floor.
  - The comparison uses exact integer cross-multiplication in `i64`, with no `Fixed` rounding.
  - An opaque tile is still seen but not seen through. Flat maps give today's answer.
- **Reach:** `depth()` becomes `sight.reach(depth, above)`.
  - `depth` is today's value (the terrain, or a light if it reaches farther).
  - `above` is the party's floor minus the lowest floor among its eight neighbours inside the map, never negative.
  - The result is capped at `MAX_VISIBILITY_DEPTH`.
  - The viewport, the automap's `look`, and remote sensing's `ray` all use it.
- **The save does not change** (`SAVE_SCHEMA` 7): heights, ramps and cliffs are static pack data. The automap
  keeps what it records today.

## Views and the JSON tier (protocol 2, additive)
- **Additions:**
  - `ViewTile.height: i16`;
  - `EdgeView::Ramp`;
  - `BlockReason::Cliff`;
  - `map.text` prints `/` for a ramp.

  A JSON client learns of a cliff from `Blocked { Cliff }`, as it learns of impassable terrain today (no op lists
  terrains; the `data.*` ops remain in the open list).
- **Docs and tests to update:** `docs/api.md` (the tables and the changelog), the drift test (`api_doc`), the
  vocabulary test, and the MCP schema proof if its numbers move.

## Client (`omnis-vector`): only what keeps it agreeing with the engine
- **Collision mirror:** `collide.rs::blocks` gains the cliff check, read from `Cell.cliffs` (not recomputed).
  `tests/agreement.rs` covers cliff edges on the fixture map.
- **Fog depth:** the fog reads the viewport's `visibility_depth`, not the current terrain's. Drawing the ground at
  its height, relief, rock faces and the camera following the ground are M8.5 step 4.
- **To check first, a possible bug:** the client's fog seems to ignore a light (a torch). If so, the player sees fog
  at the terrain's depth while the engine records farther. If confirmed, it is fixed here with a red-first test.

## Test data
- **A fixture pack `crates/omnis-sim/tests/packs/heights`** (loaded with base, not shipped). It holds a small map
  with:
  - a slope within the allowance;
  - a cliff;
  - a one-way edge (scree to stair);
  - a ramp up a terrace;
  - a ramp at the cap;
  - a hill that hides a valley;
  - a tower whose top extends the reach.
- **The shipped `packs/test` maps are unchanged in this step.** A heighted map you can walk comes with step 4, when
  the client draws height. Until then a cliff on a flat-drawn map would confuse more than it shows.
- **Replays:** adding `sight.reach` to the base pack changes its hash. If the golden fingerprints move, it must be the
  hash alone. To check, I replay at the parent commit and at the new one and compare the event streams, which must
  be identical. Only then are the fingerprints rebaselined, with the reason recorded.

## Tests (real packs, no mocks)
- **Data:**
  - heights parse, and their errors are named;
  - cliffs derive correctly: the slope, a cliff, one-way, a ramp, a ramp over the cap refused;
  - the bad-pack rows.
- **Sim:**
  - a step up a slope succeeds;
  - a step at a cliff gives `Blocked { Cliff }`, and the turn and minutes match the other refusals;
  - the one-way edge goes one way only;
  - a ramp crosses 16 units;
  - a hill hides the valley, and the same cells are seen from the hilltop;
  - the tower's reach is deeper by `above / 8`.
- **Flat equality:** on every shipped map, for every pair of cells, the new `line_of_sight` equals the old one. The old
  one is kept in the test as the reference.
- **Client:** the agreement test on the fixture map, and the fog depth test.
- **Mutations, each to be caught:**
  1. the cliff check dropped;
  2. the terrain left used in place of the terrain entered;
  3. the ramp cap skipped;
  4. sight ignoring the ground;
  5. sight ignoring a wall's height;
  6. `above` using the highest neighbour;
  7. the mirror's cliff check dropped.
