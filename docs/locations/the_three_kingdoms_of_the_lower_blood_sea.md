# The Three Kingdoms

**World:** Toel
**Continent:** Parva
**Locality:** 3 kingdoms of the lower blood sea
**Climate:** Temperate
**Biospheres:** piedmont, coastline, mountains
 

On the southern coast of the lower blood sea are three kingdoms that have reached relative stability.  Karinguard, the dwarvish town and fortress of traditional dwarvish mining and smithing that sits on a tall foothill of a large mountain range that grows off to the west into a vast set of peaks and valleys.  Shoentran is a small sea side city at the mouth of "P'ta" river that has grown up around an economy of maritime trade. Resdie is a riftborn town of refugees who have managed to carve out an agrarian living in the ruin strewed alluvial plains above an ancient lake and dam on the river.

## History
The lands of the three kingdoms are strewn with the ruins of multiple civilizations that came before the current residents.  Ancient battlefields and the debris of these previous civilizations are everywhere.  General assessments of the ruins puts the youngest at around 250 years old to some that must be thousands of years old.  Most ruins are layered on top of other ruins, with overlapping patchworks of previous inhabitants.  The most recent ruins appear to have been from a goblinoid civilization of considerable technological achievements, but older ruins appear gnomish and occasionally elvish.   

# Settlements
## Karinguard
- **Type:** City State
- **Government:** Council of Clans
- **Size:** 1k residents

### History
The dwarves of Karinguard claim to have sought out their settlement almost 200 years ago, restoring an older dwarvish outpost in the high foothills of the mountain range.  The clans that make up their people have histories of a dispersed dwarven civilization called the Denath Combine, or just Denathi, that suffered from a magical calamity in a different mountain range far south of the 3 kingdoms lands.  In their settlement histories goblins did indeed exist commonly when they arrived, but only as small nomadic tribes, far fallen from the more advanced civilization that might have existed here.  The initial 300 dwarves were able to find and restore their long lost outpost and in true dwarven fashion they restored its mines and forges and began to slowly grow.  The dwarves are self sufficient but do like to trade with their newer neighbors seeking out mostly food and drink in trade for their ores and metal works.


## Shoentran
- **Type:** City State
- **Government:** Feudal Lord
- **Size:** 2k residents
### History: 
Shoentran on the other hand claims no prior organized founding, instead having grown up from a simple fishing village some 7 generations ago.  The village slowly grew taking in ship wrecked survivors, and wanderers common from a time of heavy disturbances in the blood sea.  About 40 years ago the blood sea disturbance ended and the sailors of Shoentran began opening up trade with distant kingdoms.  A family by the name of Van Trahrun claims leadership of the city that has grown up, demanding fealty from the merchant groups that have begun to rise to prominence.  The Van Trahrun’s created the Warden organization whose continuing mission is largely regarded as the reason for the stabilization of the area, though the Warden’s consider themselves politically neutral.  The Wardens are headquartered in Shoentran but maintain chapter houses in Karinguard and Resdie.  The merchants guild is relatively young, but is the fastest growing guild in Shoentran.
 

## Resdie
- **Type:** City State
- **Government:** Wizard Council
- **Size:** 1k residents
### History:
The people of Resdie appeared from the last rift recorded in the lands of the 3 kingdoms some 50 or so years ago.  Tens of thousands of people fleeing from their homeworld through the rift from a war in their capital city.  The rift unfortunately dropped the refugees in what was considered an impassibly dangerous ruin of considerable age.  The refugees had to fight for their lives in the ruins as undead swarmed from the catacombs beneath during their arrival.  The area was considered off limits by the settlements of Karinguard and Shoentran, though wardens made their way as best they could when it became apparent what was happening.  The Resdie refugees as luck would have it had a contingent of wizards from their fallen magic school who quickly learned to take advantage of Toel’s abundant ambient mana and turned the tide against the undead of the ruins.  After months of fighting and losing over half of their population the wizards eventually overcame the undead by hunting down an ancient lich deep beneath the ruins with the help of a pair of wardens from Shoentran.  The survivors began the process of building a new settlement in the ruins which proceeded at a rapid pace thanks to their surviving wizard school.  The town quickly became a center of learning and trade ruled by the wizards but also with many noble family survivors vying for power and influence.

**NOTES:** 
- The town of Resdie has been implementing a program of clearing the surrounding ruins in the fields since their earliest days and turning the surroundings into farms.  This process is similar to the way marking of the wardens, but is destructive in nature.  Turning up the soils with both magic and tools and roughly collecting the detritus as low pile walls bordering the various fields.  These low walls surrounding the fields are processed through a cleansing ritual by the wizards school that has been rendering the surrounding land stable and of low magic taint (at least, it appears to be doing so).
- House Tourien is the last remaining noble house with a strong claim to the throne of the fallen empire from before the flight through the rift.  While previously a second tier family related to but not close to the throne, no first tier nobility remains from the previous noble structure.  The family has grown in strength and size gaining many retainers and staff from the previous first family.  They have built the second largest structure in Resdie and provide the majority of the martial services for the town.  While the eldest leadership of the house seems to understand how tenuous the refugees are in this new world and works closely with the Wizards School and the Wardens guild to strengthen the town, but the younger generations are beginning to chafe at the preimminence of the Wizards and they have their doubts on the Wardens neutrallity in Resdie affairs.
  - Very little wealth survived the retreat through the rift, the Tourien's are accutely aware of their losses and work endlessly to rebuild wealth.  They have chartered a merchants guild, work to improve trade with Shoetran and pioneered the first trade caravan to Karinguard.  While Karinguard and Shoentran are fairly insular, the Tourien's have actively been working to find other settlements and peoples to trade with.


## Foloen (uncharted)
- **Type:** Small Town
- **Government:** Elder Council
- **Size:** 400 residents
### History:
An high elf retreat that predates the last civilization collapse in the area.  The elves of this town defend the forest it's located in and do not interact with outsiders.  The elves actively hide their settlement's location, even working to remove it's knowledge from the groups that come and go in the area.  Elves who venture from the small town do not talk about where they are from and will simply refuse to speak when questioned.  Trade does occur through their allies the druids who maintain a small village at the west edge of the forest, but it is meager and usually indistinguishable from the Druids.

### The Sunken Grove (uncharted)
- **Type:** Hamlet
- **Government:** Chieftan
- **Size:** 30 residents
### History:
The sunken grove is the location of coven of druids that have existed for hundreds of years in this area, the druids settled when an ancient elven temple became abandoned hundreds of years ago.


## Places of Interest



### The Great Goblin Head
**Description:** A great stone head of some sort of goblin lies on it’s some 10 kilometers from the P’ta river.  The head is huge, 2 miles wide and sticks up half a mile out of the earth, and deeply weathered having lost most of it’s detail.  The goblins ear is one of the last obvious features left sticking up in the air like an unusually shaped and very long  spar of rock.  The head is on it’s side and buried up to it’s nose in the earth, with it’s open mouth leading into an interior cavern that exits where it is severed at its neck.

### The Old Dam
**Description:**  An ancient dam pocked with holes and crumbling from age spreads across the river P'Ta forming the dark lake.  A once vastly wide road spanned the top, but violent damage and time have made it an impassible mount of rubble slowly being drawn down to the waterway.  The damn has a hundred small holes and cracks in it where water pushes all the way through.  A sense of foreboding fills the area around the damn that is not helped at all by the moving shadows and creatures infesting the structure that are visible from a half days ride from the mega structure.
