# Introduction

Toel is vast, a world much larger than it's haibitability would normally entail.  A handful of empires and large kingdoms exist outside the 3 kingdoms.

## Jael, "The Eternal Empire"
Ancient by modern standards on Toel, the city of Jael is over ten thousand years old.  The empire has waxed and waned over the millenia, but Jael has remained unbroken through wars and cataclysms for longer than any other state near the 3 kigndoms.  But near is a relative term as the northern end of the spawling Jaelian domain is almost 1600km south of the 3 kingdoms.  Travel is possible, but extremely dangerous as the lands between are considered wild and un governable (for that matter Jaelians think the 3 kingdoms are wild and ungovernable if they have heard of them at all).

### History of The Jaelian Empire
The founding of Jael is lost to time as an almost entirely human settlement built on huge rock outcroppings in the middle of the Rheunch (flūvia Řeūnc).  Three vast natural stone arches provided natural bridges across the turbulent flow of the Rheunch to the verdant low lands on either side.  This naturally fertile and defended position served the inital founders with an tremendous advantage over many other settlements.  And centering the settlement in the river provided rare protection from wild magic swings and cataclysms. The Jaelian empire started in a good position and their empire grew with the strength of Jael at their backs.

An early alliance with the Denath Combine, two large groups of allied peoples in the eastern mountains, provided the empire with a steady trade business that grew them mightily in the early years.  The Jael were generally an orderly society that preferred trade to war, but were surrounded by mostly hostile city states in the early years.  Successive defensive wars, first with their neighbors, and later across the Aurhahl sea that the Rheunch flows into just south of Jael, slowly grew Jael into a vast empire.  Over the millenia the Jaelian Empire stabilized around the Aurhahl sea and weathered wars and cataclysms with surprising resilience.  The Denath betrayal is the last major war of the Jael that has lasted over a thousand years and is still in it's winding down years as the Jael continue their revenge campaign against their once close allies.

For the first time in the empire's history growth has finally slowed and the population is starting to decline slightly.  Most view this as a propitious natural alignment with the emporer Gustinius ascendance to a higher godhood that will raise all the Jael a step up in the world.

### The empire at a glance

The people of Jael initially were almost entirely human and their culture is still distinctly framed by those early developments. Jael has similarities to the ancient roman empire, they developed their own language variant and common is not well known in the empire.  They have developed sophisticed sciences and a broadly available education system.  Magic use in the empire is tightly regulated.  They have a large bureaucracy that keeps the govenrment stable and enhances the lives of the citizens more than it encumbers (to some extent).  The populace enjoys common entertainment and artistic endevours sponsored by the government.  And the labor market is enhanced with an extensive system of slavery, endentured servitude, and oppression to keep the favored members of the empire in power and unburdened.

The cult of the emperor, Gustinius the Ascending, is the only approved religion of the empire.  But tolerance is given, grudgingly, to other religions that serve the overall goals of the empire.  Gustinius rose to power in the empire 9,000 years ago and started his ascension to godhood shortly after that.  While he has not made a public appearence in over a thousands years all signs point to this being part of his upcoming ascension.

## Ghuaandan, "The Dragon Court"
While most of the living beings of Toel are recognizable across the multiverse, the Dragons of Toel are distinctly different.  They are all born as grey dragons, and some stay that way forever.  Over time the dragons may diversify and gain color, and colored dragons from other realms do end up on Toel, but the natives are from one common stock.  In the mountain ranges northwest of the 3 kingdoms dragons are common, with the grey dragons have formed a common government and their drakin (draconic humanoids) inhabit a handful of small cities that while connected in government are fairly isolated regionally and each have their own unique culture.  The Dragon Court is generally isolationist, but has been opening up in recent years to at least tolerate visitors, but is not an active trading group with anyone.

## Harentee, "Cities of the Sun"
An eastern federation of city states recovering from the last magic cataclsym built on an ismuth and arceopalago that separates the blood sea from the eastern ocean of Outhoca.  The Outhocan side of the empire is a hot tropical and desert strewn area, the western side is sub tropical.  The Harentee are small in population and still struggling to towards growth as numerous creatures and competing humanoids have moved into the area since the last cataclysm.  The Harantee maintain sea based trade routes with Shoentran, and several city states have expressed an interest in the new magic school in the 3 kingdoms as a possible solution to their struggles to reform as a major power.

## Souilddil, "Common Wealth of the Floating Islands"
Far west of the 3 kingdoms over the western ocean Kullin, are the marvelous floating islands of Suilddil.  The floating landmasses can reach up to 500km in size though the area is filled with smaller floating islands.  The floating islands were once a part of a large penninsula that stretched out into Kullin but during the last magic cataclysm the entire landmass was torn apart and began to float into the sky.  The existing gnomish civilization on the penninsula was destroyed, but there were some scattered survivors who teamed up with sever species of winged humanoids to begin rebuilding into an aerial civilization.
