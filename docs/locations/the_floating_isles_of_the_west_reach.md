# "Souilddil", The Floating Isles of the West Reach

**World:** Toel
**Continent:** Parva
**Locality:** 3 kingdoms of the lower blood sea
**Climate:** Temperate
**Biospheres:** mystic isles, volcanic, islands, rocky temperate coastline

## History
Once a thriving peninsula some 400km in length and averaging over a hundred wide, this land is one of the most dramatic casualties of the last cataclysm.  At some point the peninsula was shattered by a titanic event and the whole area of the ocean was enshrouded in mist and fog.
