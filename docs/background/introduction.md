The world of Omnis, Toel, is both new and old, the players begin as members of the latest wave of settlers who have arrived or are decended from people who travelled across massive portals that occassionally open spanning worlds.  Omnis is a vast place filled with danger and riches, a tempting world to migrate to with opportunity everywhere.  

## About Time
Strange magical forces are present everywhere with the world virtually humming with reality twisting forces.  Even time itself is constantly twisting and changing by the dynamic forces only coverging briefly between folk as they interact with one another.  So much so that the standard greatings are time relative questions like:

    "Hallo, been n'long" (Hello, it has not been long since I've seen you.)

Would be responded to with the time consolidation counter greeting:

    "Hallo, been moons on dae road." (Hello, I've been on the road for months since last I saw you.)

Dramatic changes from meeting to meeting of people are just accepted as part of life in Omnis.

Ancient civilizations that lie in ruins across the world are sometimes encountered live and well as time twists around travellers.  Apocolypses from forgotten ages return occassionally.  Towns, kingdoms and whole civilizations dissappear or appear overnight.  While there is a general stability to the world and it's denizens, it is never guaranteed.  Some magic wielders even take advantage of the flux to achieve things impossible on any other world.

## An Unstable world

Toel sits at a conjunction of powerful magic currents across the multiverse but it is not a stable junction, the world is constantly in flux due to currents that flow through it.  Like the chaotic counterpart to the city of doors all places in the multiverse are near, but unlike the city of doors only the desparate and adventurous choose, but more often are forced to travel to Toel.  Toel sits in an sort of gravity well in the metaphysical planes, it is easy to enter, difficult to escape.  The world has pulled in tremendous magical energies and has unparalleled riches.

While the collection of power and wealth attracts the bold, a phenomena known as the rifts brings even more people to the world.  Rifts are short lived(usually) portals, often one way, that open into Toel from other planes and worlds.  Most often they appear during violent or chaotic magical events of tremendous force on their origin world.  Quite often the result in cataclysmic wars, large magical experiments gone awry, or the warring of the gods across the planes.  These rifts usually offer an escape for peoples and creatures across the realms, usually the only escape from an otherwise tragic end where they are spawned.  Toel is not the peaceful escape many of these rift refugees want, but it is an escape to better odds, usually.

Toel is sometimes referred to as the ladder to godhood due to its abundance of magic and how the general instability also makes it a flexible place to seek ascension.  This has generally attracted more evil and power hungry risk takers than people of good intentions over the ages.  Civilizations rise and fall commonly to the follies of ascension, people of more noble intent and commoners just trying to get by are often left scrambling and scrapping across an often war ravaged landscape.  Toel is also a place of enourmous beauty and serinity in the calm between the storms, it's lanscape littered with monuments to powerful individuals and civilizations and magic embued plants and wildlife.

The stories of the people of Toel are often shot through with adventure and heroism.  The struggle for life and prosperity amonst the sscension struggles makes for a strong and determined common folk.
