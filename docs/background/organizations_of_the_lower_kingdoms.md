# Introduction
The three kingdoms is a group of settlements in recovery from the last cataclysm to pass through this region.  Guilds and mission oriented groups have come into being to support the people across the three kingdoms.

## The Wardens
The town of Shoetran is home to the Wardens, a group of rangers and druids who are dedicated to restoring the land since the last cataclysm.  The group claims to have been in existence in the fishing village of Shoentran as the town wood cutters who had early interactions with the druids of the middle wood.  Their mission evolving as the town grew to initially protect the town and establish trade with the Druids.  When the dwarves of Karinguard became known the wardens were the first to blaze a trail through the dangerous Kak forest establishing a trade route.  When the major rift appeared, two wardens scouting on the goblin head ruined witnessed the event and petitioned Shoentran to send help to the large group of refugees.  The wardens were a part of the desparate plunge into the catacombs and ruins beneath Resdie by the remainng nobles and wizards to defeat the lich prince and turn the undead hordes.  While Shoentran is their home, the residents of Resdie warmly provide for the wardens and a warden school has grown up beside the resurrected wizards school.  The close partnership with Resdie has provided the wardens with horses, making them one of the only groups commonly operating far from Resdie on horseback in the 3 kingdoms.
