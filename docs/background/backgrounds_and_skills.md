# Backgrounds

These are some of the backgrounds for characters in Toel

## Explorer

A common vocation in a largely unsettled world, explorers often have their own guilds or schools of formal training.  Experts in survival and scouting of the wilds, and knowledgeable in how to avoid the many dangers.  Explorers are often suitable fighters, hunters and know a little or sometimes a lot about magic and nature.  Cartography and orienteering are major skills for explorers.  This background is suitable for most character types.

### Proficiencies

* Cartography
* Orienteering

### Bonus Equipment

- Map making kit:
  - small ruler
  - writing compass
  - sextant
  - 10 sheets of paper
  - 3 quill pins
  - travel inkpot(full, 20 uses)
  - waxed carrying tube

## Prospector
A common vocation in a world rich with mineral wealth (an oddity given the number of ancient ruined civilizations present on Toel).  Experts in mining and extracting minerals from the environment, prospectors are engineers of significant training.  Mining, field assayment and simple refining are core skills.

### Proficiencies

* Mining
* Assaying
* Refining

## Warden
One of the new backgrounds for settlers and populations is the need for a type of protector against monsters and magic anomalies.  Typically rangers and paladins take this background, but it is not restricted to any character.  Wardens range freely as investigators and enforcers for their patrons or sometimes work as mercenaries.  They search for and find magic and monster related problems near settlements and in the vicinity of nation states.  Sometimes they organize responses, other times they deal with the problems themselves.

### Proficiencies

* Arcana
* Investigation

# Proficiencies

These skills expand on the basic set of skills derived from the SRD and provide different in game capabilities.

## Cartography

The skill of making maps that accurately detail your surroundings while traveling and make good approximations of where places beyond your point of view exist.  In game this skill has two primary effects: 
1. It provides a party with records perception check observations and journal entries for observations interacted with adding a new level of detail maps.
2. Every sector map recieves a cartography modifier to fast travel encounter avoidance, this bonus is determined by a background cartography check when leaving an existing sector.
### User Triggered
The player can trigger the skill to spend time producing high a high quality map of the current sector if they have paper avaialable.  These maps have value as trafe and or quest items.

## Orienteering

The skill of navigating by maps, tools, and landmarks.  This skill improves several aspects of travel in the game:
1. It provides a bonus to travel encounter checks.
2. It provides a bonus to survival checks.
3. It provides a bonus to travel speed.
### User Triggered
When triggered the orienteering skill can be used to attempt to find nearby terrain features, such as water sources, inhabitations, caves, landscape features, monsters, food sources, crafting resources sources.  The list of what can be looked for depends on character/skill level.  An animated marker is dropped on the map to indicate general direction of where the character has determined where the best place to search for such a things.
