# Backgrounds

These are some of the backgrounds for characters in Toel

## Explorer

A common vocation in a largely unsettled world, explorers often have their own guilds or schools of formal training.  Experts in survival and scouting of the wilds, and knowledgeable in how to avoid the many dangers.  Explorers are often suitable fighters, hunters and know a little about magic and nature.  Cartography and investigation are major skills for explorers

### Proficiencies

* Cartography
* Orienteering

# Skills

These skills expand on the basic set of skills derived from the SRD and provide different in game capabilities.

## Cartography

The skill of making maps that accurately detail your surroundings while traveling and make good approximations of where places beyond your point of view exist.  In game this skill has two primary effects: 
1. It provides a party with records perception check observations and journal entries for observations interacted with adding a new level of detail maps.
2. Every sector map recieves a cartography modifier to fast travel encounter avoidance, this bonus is determined by a background cartography check when leaving an existing sector.

## Orienteering

The skill of navigating by maps, tools, and landmarks.
