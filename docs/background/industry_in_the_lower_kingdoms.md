# Industry and the problem with Magic
Since Toel is an unstable high magic world, the normal path to industrialization through magic is not safe for settlements and civilizations.  The leaders of the kingdoms value magic neutral approaches such as beasts of burden and mechanical solutions.

# Domesticated Animal Labor

The 3 kingdoms looks tranquilic from a distance, but it is an area only relatively recently recovered from one of the devestating cataclysms so common on Toel.  Common live stocks were mostly wiped out, with only chickens and goats are commonly raised among the survivoring settlements.  The three kingdoms have each filled in the typical roles for beasts of burden and labor in interesting ways.  The settlement of Resdie stands apart though as a refugee group the greater rift that brought them to Toel also carried cattle and horses, though the numbers of their livestock are just now recovering and either is rare outside of Resdie itself.

## Sheep variants
The halflings of the lower kingdoms have been widely successful at raising horned sheep, though there is no history of how they came across sheep in the recovering area of the 3 kingdoms.  The halflings sheep are mostly similar to the human domesticated variety, though the males have been bread to be stronger and bigger than normal.  

## Toil Rams and Battle Rams
The rams are basically broken down into 2 varieties: "Toil Rams"(sometimes shortened to 'torams') are the beasts of burden strong enough to replace 6 halflings in strength intensive work; and "Battle Rams" capable of carrying a rider and with a temperment suitable for combat.  The rams signature headbutt is unchanged as a way they attack foes and the halfling riders have adapted their weapons and tactics to take advantage of the natural headbutt attack.

## Oxen variants
The dwarves of Karinguard travelled by ox drawn caravans in their flight from Denathi.  These dwarven oxen are smaller and stouter that common varietes, capable of tremendous strength and endurance.  Being of dwarven domestication they also happen to have darkvision and are not spooked by being far underground or in enclosed spaces at all.  Unique to their subspecies, these dwarven oxen have forward facing horns that curve under their eyes and the extend as a pair reaching cewntrally up and over their noses almost a foot beyond their snouts.  The oxen horns are thicker, and stronger than regular oxen horn offering the oxen a strong weapon and a utilitarian appendage good for digging and moving things.  Also to compensate for their obstructed snouts the oxen have very long, very prehensile tongues.

### War Oxen
The dwarves breed some of their oxen as war mounts that are as formidable as any war horse, especially when clad in mail armor and admantine tipped horns.

# Torsioncraft
An ancient technology kept alive by both the Dwarves in Karinguard and the Gnomes in Shoentran where mechanical force is captured in mundane pressure based mechanisms and utilized with gears, pistons and levers.  Unlike many of the lost magical arts that succumb to the divine wars that criss cross the surface of Toel periodically, torsioncraft only uses magic to convert energy into a storable form in often non-magical apparatus.  Being mundane in implementation means examples of this technology haven't suffered the extreme magical onslaughts of the turbulent world.  Torsioncraft apparati sometimes survive vast periods of time still working or can be repaired or replicated far more easily than destroyed or empty magic items.

## Hybrid Mechanisms
While the basics of Torsioncraft produce mundane mechnisms with stored potential power, hybrid systems use magical energy to provide even more power and capability.  Hybrid systems have all the failings of magical advancements on Toel such advanced mechanisms and apparati are often lost to time often to be rediscovered by new civilizations.  

The ruins of great hybrid mechanisms and apparati are everywhere, their workings and purposes are often truly mysterious to the current inhabitants.

# Hylomancy
A magical school of animating basic or prime substances, similar to Golems or animated statues.  This school of magic is impractical on most worlds as being too magical energy intensive to use widely, but on Toel this is commonly explored area of endevour.  The abundance of magical energy makes this school one of the most commonly rediscovered schools in rising civilizations on Toel.  Advancing civilizations often adopt magically animated constructs to do all manner of work.

## Hylosomatics and Hyloplasty
The study and act of binding animated matter to biological systems is a regularly recurring theme in maagic on Toel.  Learned and lost numerous times, different civilizations and groups have explored strange amalgams of people and construct.  Though an easy enough path to explore once Hylomancy starts to become more advanced, Hylosomatics has stability risk that often carries it to ruin during periods of cataclysm.

# High Smithing
An ancient art that the dwarves of Karinguard have preserved, high smithing is the art of using magic to build with tightly controlled and purified metals and minerals.  The outputs are generally mundane but of a quality not possible in the general scientific and industrial level of the world.  Perfectly pure and hardened or softened steel, exotic metal alloys, bronzes of unfathomable strength, diamond grown into natural tool shapes, and many more outputs are possible.  The outputs of the high smiths of Karinguard are deeply valued for their attributes and their magic neutral properties.
